## Verdicts

- **Functionality: pass** for the bounded scope on bundle `BKiZRnVN`: options are planner-grounded, unavailable options fail closed, preparation never mutates the draft, stale context is rejected, M2b F1–F3 behaviour holds.
- **Visual clarity: revise.** M3-1, M3-2, M3-3 and M3-5 prevent an operator from stating the consequence from the main view in the identity and lifecycle cases without opening engine JSON.
- **AI helpfulness: not exercised** with a live agent. Fixture options are implementer-authored; the lifecycle preview's silence about undistinguishing samples (M3-3) is a product gap, not a fixture gap. F04/F5 remain open.
- **Accessibility (tested scope): pass** for keyboard activation, focus after prepare and Clear, and 430 px reflow; the disabled Prepare and Explain buttons lack a programmatic link to their reason (M3-5). No screen-reader run or WCAG claim.
