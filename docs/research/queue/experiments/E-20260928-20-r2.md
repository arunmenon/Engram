claim: This is a conditional pilot. On the 10 conversations of LoCoMo, replacing our hosted extractor with a self-hosted open-weight extractor of about 31B parameters (gemma-4-31b-it) lowers answer accuracy by no more than 2 points when the retrieval, the served-token budget, the answering model and the judge are held fixed. The inference is conditional on these 10 conversations: the primary interval resamples questions, and a conversation-cluster interval is reported beside it; with 10 clusters no conversation-robust claim is made. PDLC seed questions screen only for a large loss; no conclusion about PDLC non-inferiority at 2 points is drawn. The claim concerns where memory text goes at write time only: in arm B the reader and the judge are still hosted (see data exposure below). Source: N-20260928-27 (VarOps Acropolis Technical Report 01, repo b391c09, harness 93ad8b6): with gemma-4-31b-it on one H200 building the memory record and gemini-3.7-flash reading, scores were within 1 to 2 points of all-frontier (LongMemEval-S 89.6 vs 91.4, LoCoMo 81.8 vs 81.8, BEAM 100K 55.2 vs 56.2); local loss sat in the reading seats and grew with length (2.2, 5.5, 8.0 points). Caveats: the frontier model was also the judge; placement rows were single runs against a noise floor of about 1 point; "own everything" was the better of two local configurations; the seat split was measured on BEAM 100K only; all three datasets are conversational; a vendor evaluated its own product.

arms (one frozen memory pipeline; same corpus, retrieval, served-token budget of 3,000 tokens, answering prompt and judge; only the extractor, and in arm C also the reader, differs):
- A (baseline): the production hosted extractor (pinned model and prompt) with the pinned hosted reader; two independent builds, A1 and A2.
- B: gemma-4-31b-it (pinned revision) self-hosted on one GPU via vLLM as the extractor, same extraction prompt and output schema, pinned hosted reader; two independent builds, B1 and B2.
- C (all-local, reported, not decided on): B1's memory with gemma-4-31b-it as reader, to price the answer seat, without best-of-two selection.
- override: the card asks for 3 runs per arm; this spec runs 2 builds per arm for A and B (each build adds about 4.9M tokens with its answers and judging). Two builds per arm give a build-to-build noise estimate for both arms, which is what REV-20260928-42 asked for; a third would take the spec to about 18 runs.

metric: answer accuracy (primary) per question, averaged over the arm's two builds (values 0, 0.5, 1), graded by a judge from a model family different from every reader and extractor (not Gemini, not Gemma, not the hosted extractor's or reader's family), with a 10% human-checked subsample; answerable-only accuracy (categories 1 to 4) and correct abstention on adversarial questions (category 5) reported separately; build noise |A1 minus A2| and |B1 minus B2|; extraction cost per 1,000 writes, GPU hours and extraction latency.

predeclared margin: LoCoMo non-inferiority: the lower end of the two-sided 95% question-level interval of (B minus A) above minus 2 points (the card's margin); the conversation-cluster interval is reported and, if its lower end is also above minus 2, the result is flagged `conversation_robust`. PDLC large-harm screen: harm is flagged when (B minus A) is below minus 5 points and its 95% interval lies wholly below zero.

sample size and power note: LOCOMO-600: 120 questions from each of the five categories, drawn with seed 20261026 from the pinned release. Assuming a paired discordance of about 0.10 per build and a correlation of about 0.6 between builds, the SE of the build-averaged difference is about sqrt(0.10 / 600) x sqrt(0.8) = 1.15 points. At a true difference of 0, the lower bound clears minus 2 points with probability Phi(2 / 1.15 minus 1.96) = Phi(-0.22), about 0.41; at a true loss of 5 points, harm is shown (point below minus 2 and upper bound below zero) with probability about 0.99. Correction to E-20260928-20: under its own assumptions (SE 0.81, two-sided 95% rule) the pass probability at equality was Phi(2 / 0.81 minus 1.96) = Phi(0.51), about 0.70, not 0.80. The 10 conversations are shared by every question, so the question-level interval is conditional on them; the conversation-cluster bootstrap with 10 clusters undercovers and is reported, not decided on. PDLC: 120 questions, two builds per arm, SE about 2.5 to 3 points; it can flag a large loss and nothing finer.

kill criterion: negative if the LoCoMo point estimate of (B minus A) is below minus 2 points and its 95% interval lies wholly below zero, or if the PDLC large-harm flag is raised. A point estimate below minus 2 whose interval reaches zero is inconclusive, not negative. Invalid (rerun under a new spec revision, not a verdict): judge agreement with the human subsample below 0.85; |A1 minus A2| or |B1 minus B2| above 3 points on LOCOMO-600 (the pipeline is too noisy for a 2-point question); any served-token budget differing between arms.

what a pass unlocks: a lead-grade, conditional result for the priority 2 goal at the ingestion stage (memory text stays in-house at write time, with the answer step still hosted); a sized confirmatory run; a measured price of the answer seat (arm C) for any later all-local plan.

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): extraction: about 1.0M tokens per build (LoCoMo about 0.5M with prompt overhead, PDLC about 0.5M), 4 builds, 4.0M (2.0M self-hosted); answering: 720 questions per build (600 LoCoMo, 120 PDLC) x 3.5k tokens = 2.5M per build, 4 builds with the hosted reader = 10.1M, plus arm C 2.5M self-hosted; judging: 5 answer sets x 720 x 0.8k = 2.9M. Total about 19.5M tokens, 13 runs at no more than 1.5M each. USD at the planning ceiling (3 USD per million input tokens, 15 USD per million output tokens for hosted models; 4 USD per GPU-hour): hosted extraction about 12 USD, hosted reading about 34 USD, judging about 9 USD, about 6 GPU-hours about 24 USD; total about 79 USD, cap 90 USD. Harness 4 engineer-days (the PDLC questions are shared with E-20260928-18-r2). Cost M.
