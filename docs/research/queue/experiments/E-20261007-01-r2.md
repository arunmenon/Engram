claim: This is an exploratory comparison of complete extraction pipelines on our small PDLC schema (7 node types; trace links implements, verifies, refines, satisfies, blocks), at a common per-artifact call budget. Primary: extracting without the schema in the prompt, then canonicalising by embedding and repairing violations (B), is compared with the schema in the prompt followed by the identical canonicalise, check and repair stages (A2), on pooled trace-link F1 and precision against an adjudicated gold, and on tokens per artifact. A one-pass schema-in-prompt arm (A1) is the current-practice reference. Secondary, decided separately: a System One model making the ambiguity and repair decisions (C) against B, with direct protection against A1. Source: N-SURVEY-20261006-02 (OAK+MEND, arXiv 2605.29168, primary HTML Tables 1 and 2): Qwen schema-valid triples 96.8% against 79.4% for In-Context; Table 1 normalised weighted costs 0.59 against 0.79, weighted at a prompt-to-completion ratio of 1/4, which is about a 25% weighted saving against In-Context (the card title's 40% is against Wikontic); HotpotQA F1 56.4 against 55.6 are comparable point estimates, not a demonstrated equivalence. The source's in-context baseline selects top-k ontology fragments after an open predicate pass over thousands of types; it does not paste a whole small ontology, so the source motivates the question, not a transferable cost advantage. N-SURVEY-20261006-03 numbers (Mistral 7B triple F1 4.2; 61.5% of one model's triples absent from gold) are unverified pending the ACL PDF and are not used for any margin.

arms:
- A1 (current-practice reference): extractor model M, full schema with domain/range rules inlined in the prompt, one pass, no canonicalisation or repair.
- A2 (matched baseline): A1's prompt, then the same canonicalise, symbolic check and repair stages as B, under the same call caps.
- B: model M, no schema in the prompt; types and predicates canonicalised by cosine similarity (Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B, pinned revision) to schema labels; candidates within beta 0.05 of the best go to M as a single multiple-choice question; symbolic domain/range check; M repairs violating links.
- C: as B, but the ambiguity choice and each repair are decided by hosted Jev (pinned) as one yes/no Noul per candidate link type.
- Common rules for A2, B and C: at most 1 extraction call, 4 disambiguation calls and 2 repair rounds per artifact; candidate list limited to the top 3 by similarity; if no candidate scores at least 0.60 the triple is dropped (no-match); ties broken by schema order; entity identity matched by normalised node title plus artifact id, with no fuzzy merge. In C, if Jev answers yes to more than one candidate the highest-probability yes wins; if all are no, the triple is dropped; a link still violating domain/range after 2 repair rounds is dropped in every arm.
- M is pinned by version string, temperature 0, seed 7, max output 1,200 tokens per call; rendering and chunking identical across arms. Prompts are frozen files hashed in the manifest.

metric: primary estimand: pooled link-level micro F1 over all 300 test artifacts (exact source node, target node, link type), artifacts with empty gold contributing their false positives. Secondary: pooled precision and recall, per-repository macro F1, schema-valid rate, input plus output tokens per artifact, calls per artifact, p95 latency per artifact. Statistic: paired differences B minus A2, B minus A1, C minus B and C minus A1; 95% cluster bootstrap CI (10,000 resamples, seed 7), resampling repositories, then artifacts within repository.

predeclared margin: B decision: no large loss if the lower 95% bound of F1 (B minus A2) is above -0.05 and of precision (B minus A2) is above -0.05, with mean tokens per artifact B/A2 at or under 0.80 and its upper bound at or under 0.90. C decision (separate): C may take the ambiguity and repair slot only if the lower bound of F1 (C minus B) is above -0.03, of precision (C minus B) above -0.03, and of F1 (C minus A1) above -0.03, so C cannot drift 6 points below current practice.

sample size and power note: 300 artifacts in 8 repositories. Using the earlier assumption (per-artifact F1 SD 0.25, paired correlation 0.6), the unclustered 95% half-width is about 2.5 points; with 8 clusters it widens, so the run is exploratory and the margins are set at 5 points for B. Before scoring, `power_sim.py` resamples the 8 repositories from development-free pilot noise (A1 versus A1 with a different seed on the 30 development artifacts) to report the half-width at 8 clusters and the cluster count a 3-point confirmatory test needs; that number sizes the follow-up spec.

kill criterion: interim (after artifacts 1 to 100, cost only, no F1 look): stop if mean tokens per artifact for B is at or above A2's, since the saving cannot then reach the margin. Final: kill B if the upper 95% bound of F1 (B minus A2) is below -0.03, or token ratio B/A2 above 0.90.

what a pass unlocks: a confirmatory spec on more repositories for removing the schema from PDLC extraction prompts; a separately justified slot for the System One model in the priority 3 narrow slice; cleaner typed links for H7.

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): 1 run. Calls per artifact at most: A1 1, A2 7, B 7, C 7 (with up to 4 Jev decisions each covering up to 3 candidates). Typical case about 2.5 calls per artifact for A2, B and C. Worst-case tokens are bounded by the reservation rule; expected about 1.7M tokens and under 10 USD at prices pinned in the manifest. People-days: 2 for gold adjudication (two annotators, a third resolves), 1.5 for harness and scorer.
