claim: For an open-weight instruct model used as a memory reranker (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, pinned, self-hosted), scoring each candidate separately (pointwise) gives higher recall@1 than one listwise call over the same pool by at least 5 points at pool size 20, the reverse of what B10 found for Jev's choice call; and at pool size 200 no LLM rerank arm beats no rerank on recall@5.

Sources and what they do and do not show. N-SURVEY-20261003-07 (recommendation reranking study): at K=20 Jev's single choice call had the best MRR (0.238 / 0.295 / 0.346 on Movies, Video Games, Books), Qwen2.5-7B pointwise was close (0.232 / 0.259 / 0.282) and Qwen listwise was worst (0.199 / 0.213 / 0.245); at K=200 on Movies no LLM arm beat SASRec without rerank (MRR 0.093 vs Jev 0.080). Limits: recommendation, not memory; one held-out item as ground truth; users filtered to targets inside SASRec's top 200; no cross-encoder; call counts and cost not stated; latency not hardware-normalised; text and tables disagree on large-K claims (table values used). The card's numbers match. Applicability to memory is low, which is why this is the exploration slot and is sized as a cheap pilot on our data. E-20260926-06-r2 compares Jev's choice call against local cross-encoders and per-candidate yes/no questions in one request for Jev; it has no open instruct-model listwise vs pointwise arm and no pool sweep beyond its fused pool, so its arms cannot answer this claim; not merged.

arms (same candidate pools per question from the pinned hybrid retriever, same memory text truncated to 120 tokens per item, same question text):
- R (baseline): no rerank, retriever order.
- J: hosted Jev (pinned version), one choice call with the K items as alternatives, scores = returned probabilities.
- L: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct listwise, one call returning a ranked list of item ids (frozen parser; unparsed ids appended in retriever order, counted).
- P: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct pointwise, one call per item, score = P("yes") from next-token distribution restricted to yes/no and renormalised.
- pool sizes: K in {20, 50, 200}; K=20 on all 160 questions, K=50 on the 100 LoCoMo questions, K=200 on 50 LoCoMo questions (P on 30 of them); PDLC pools rarely exceed 100 candidates, recorded.

metric: primary, recall@1 (gold memory ranked first) per arm and K; recall@5; MRR; calls per question; tokens; p95 latency (Jev hosted including network, open model on the pinned GPU host, reported separately and not compared head-to-head).

predeclared margin: claim 1 (call shape) positive needs recall@1(P) minus recall@1(L) at K=20, pooled over both datasets, at least 0.05 with the 95% cluster-bootstrap lower bound above 0; negative needs recall@1(L) minus recall@1(P) at K=20 with lower bound above 0. Claim 2 (large pool) is supported if, at K=200, every LLM arm's recall@5 is at most recall@5(R) plus 0.02 (point estimate); refuted if any arm's paired difference over R has lower bound above 0. J is reported against L and P to place the open model relative to B10's evidence.

sample size and power note: 100 LoCoMo-200 questions (sampled with seed 20261116, about 10 conversations) and 60 PDLC-SEED-ASF questions (about 15 Decisions or components) at K=20 (K=50 and K=200 subsets as in the arms list). Clustering unit: conversation or component. At discordance 0.25 between P and L and design effect 1.5, SE of the K=20 paired difference is about 0.048, so the lower bound above 0 clears with probability about 0.5 at a true 5-point gap and about 0.85 at the note's 3 to 10 point gaps' upper end; this is a pilot that sizes a confirmatory run. `tl power` runs before any model call.

kill criterion: negative for claim 1 as above. Invalid: pools differing between arms; truncation or prompts differing from the pin; open model weights hash or Jev version differing; L's unparsed-id rate above 10%; per-arm failure rate above 3%; fewer than 150 questions scored in all arms at K=20.

what a pass unlocks: the call shape and pool cap for a self-hosted reranker (priority 2: run our own System One model so memory text stays in-house); B10 (lead) is either narrowed to Jev or confirmed for an open model; the pool size beyond which reranking stops paying, which caps the rerank stage whatever model owns it.

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): harness 2 engineer-days (pools, three rerank adapters, scorer); no labelling (LoCoMo gold evidence; PDLC seed questions carry gold Decision ids from H7's fixture). Tokens: listwise per question about 2.6k (K=20), 6.4k (K=50), 25k (K=200); pointwise about K x 0.2k; Jev about listwise size. To fit the 7M cap the design is: K=20 on all 160 questions; K=50 on the 100 LoCoMo questions only; K=200 for J and L on 50 LoCoMo questions and for P on 30 (reported, not decided). Planned: J 1.0M, L 1.0M, P 1.6M at K=20 and 50; J 1.25M, L 1.25M, P 1.2M at K=200; total about 7.3M planned before truncation savings, measured with the pinned tokenizer at freeze; if the measured total exceeds 7M, P at K=200 is dropped first and recorded. Six runs. USD: Jev about 10, open model GPU time on the harness host. Cost S.
