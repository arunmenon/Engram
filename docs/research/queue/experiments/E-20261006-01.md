claim: At the H5 write gate, with identical Jev instructions, a projection that only prunes fields and caps list lengths (no derived labels) is non-inferior to raw item plus context on F1 of accepted items (margin 3 points) and cuts mean input tokens by at least 25%; a projection that adds labels from thresholds fitted on the fitting half only does not beat prune-only by more than 3 points F1 on the test half. Together these separate the portable gain (trimming noise) from the non-portable gain (pre-computing the answer) that inflates jev-state's 74.3% to 100.0% (N-SURVEY-20261005-01, repo-pinned at 3c30e89, where the projection bands use the exact cuts and words of the label rules).

Relation to open work: E-20261001-03-r2 tests projection on staleness and supersession decisions and has no leakage-control arm. This spec is a different stage (write gate) and adds the prune-only arm and fit-half-only thresholds, so the same arms would not answer both. Not merged.

arms:
- A (baseline): raw extracted item plus its source context, all fields, H5 Jev request (Noul `quote_supports_item`, Choice `source_type`, Score `strength`), H5 instructions unchanged.
- B (prune-only): same request and instructions; fields the three questions do not use are dropped, lists capped at 5 by recency; no banding, no derived labels.
- C (fitted-label): B plus banded labels for numeric fields (age, citation count, quote overlap) with cut points fitted on the fitting half by maximising F1 against fitting-half labels; cut points frozen before the test half is touched.

metric: precision, recall and F1 of accepted items against labels on the test half; ECE by probability band (10 bands) per arm; mean input tokens per call; p95 latency.

predeclared margin: B non-inferior to A if the lower 95% bound of (F1_B minus F1_A) is above -0.03; token cut at least 25% (B vs A). C counts as a gain over B only if the lower bound of (F1_C minus F1_B) is above 0.

sample size and power note: 90 items, split by project and time into 45 fit and 45 test. With 45 test items the paired F1 interval will be wide (roughly plus or minus 0.10); this run can show harm or a large gain, not fine non-inferiority. Three reruns per item are reported for stability only and do not add to n. If the interval spans the margin, outcome is inconclusive, not pass.

kill criterion: kill prune-only if the upper 95% bound of (F1_B minus F1_A) is below -0.03, or if the token cut is under 25%. Kill fitted-label if ECE_C on the test half is above 0.15 (H5's ECE bar).

what a pass unlocks: a cheap projection step at the write gate that also reduces Jev cost per decision; evidence on B14 (calibration on our data with and without projection).

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): 3 arms x 90 items x 3 reruns = 810 Jev calls, under 1.5M input tokens, well under 5 USD; 1 people-day to write the projection for B and the fitting code for C; 0.5 day to analyse.
