claim: Cross-fitted isotonic recalibration of the save/skip (write admission) and injection-gate probabilities, fitted per source (chat turns, repo docs, tool output), lowers per-source ECE by at least 2x and raises precision at fixed 80% coverage by at least 3 points compared with fixed thresholds on raw probabilities.

arms:
- Arm A: raw decision-model probabilities with fixed thresholds (save/skip 0.5, injection 0.5).
- Arm A2 (added control): raw probabilities with a single threshold fitted on the fitting half, pooled across sources. Separates "fit a threshold locally" (already H5's recipe, B14) from "recalibrate per source", so a pass is attributable to recalibration and not to threshold fitting.
- Arm B: isotonic recalibration fitted per source on one half of the labels, scored on the other half, halves swapped, repeated 20 times with fresh random splits (stratified by label within source); threshold chosen on the fitting half for 80% coverage.
- Arm B2 (secondary): Platt scaling with the same protocol, since isotonic on about 75 items per fold can overfit.

metric: per-source ECE (15 equal-mass bins), calibration slope, Brier score, and precision at 80% coverage on the scoring half.

predeclared margin: pass if ECE(A)/ECE(B) >= 2 on at least 2 of 3 sources for each gate AND precision at 80% coverage of B exceeds A2 (not only A) by >= 3 points. Precision against A is reported but does not decide.

sample size and power note: at least 150 labelled items per source per gate (900 total), labelled by someone other than the gate author (B21 sizes). With 75 items per scoring half, ECE estimates carry a standard error of roughly 0.02 to 0.03, which is comparable to the ECE values in N-20260926-07 (0.012 to 0.046); the 20 repeated cross-fits average split noise but not label-set noise, so the result is reported with a bootstrap over items and should be read as a per-source estimate, not a population claim. Note that only one of the three sources the card cites (N-20260926-07) was supplied as a note; the other two (RLCDAlignBench, Banking77) were not checked.

kill criterion: ECE ratio < 1.3 on all sources for both gates, OR precision change of B versus A2 within +/- 1 point on both gates. Stop before running if any source has fewer than 100 labelled items for a gate (record as not runnable).

what a pass unlocks: a per-source recalibration step in the gate recipe for H5, H10b and any later gate; thresholds set by coverage target rather than a vendor-scale 0.5. Moves B14 (working) with our own measurement and informs B13 (cascade thresholds depend on calibrated tiers).

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): M. No new inference beyond the H5 and H10b shadow runs (probabilities are logged there); the cost is label collection above what H5 and H10b already require (up to 900 items if their sets are not split by source) and about 1 people-day for analysis.
