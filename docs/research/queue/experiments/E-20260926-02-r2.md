claim: On our labelled escalation set, the input-feature escalation classifier's within-repository AUC under leave-one-repository-out (LORO) evaluation is at least 0.10 below its within-repository AUC under session-grouped cross-validation, and its session-grouped signal does not exceed a within-repository label-permutation null (permutation p >= 0.05); separately, a deferral model on tier-1 output features has at least 0.05 higher LORO within-repository AUC than the input-feature classifier, with the repository-clustered interval excluding zero.

arms:
- Arm A (session-grouped CV): the H5 input-feature escalation classifier, 5-fold cross-validation with folds grouped by session (turns of one session never straddle folds) and stratified by label. The only difference from Arm A-LORO is whether repositories are shared between train and test.
- Arm A-LORO: same classifier, features and hyperparameters, trained on all repositories but one and scored on the held-out repository, for each qualifying repository.
- Arm A-perm (permutation null): Arm A's session-grouped protocol with training-fold labels permuted within repository (each repository's base rate preserved), 200 permutations; every evaluation uses the untouched true test labels.
- Baseline G-item (group-rate, session-grouped): predicts each test item's repository training-fold escalation rate. Its pooled AUC measures how much AUC repository identity alone buys when repositories are shared across folds.
- Baseline G-LORO (constant): under LORO, the pooled training rate, constant within the held-out repository; its within-repository AUC is 0.5 by construction and it is reported only to make that explicit.
- Arm B: deferral model on tier-1 output features (answer length in tokens, abstain or hedge markers, confidence spread across tier-1 components), logistic regression, scored LORO.

metric: primary statistic for every arm: within-repository AUC = mean over qualifying repositories of the per-repository AUC, weighted by (positives x negatives) in that repository. Pooled out-of-fold AUC is reported but never decides, because pooling mixes fold priors. Label: 1 if the tier-2 decision differs from tier 1 and matches the gold label. Secondary: permutation p-value; pooled AUC of G-item; label test-retest kappa on a 50-item blind relabel.

predeclared margin: group_memorisation requires ALL of: (auc_A - auc_A_loro) >= 0.10 with the repository-clustered 95% interval excluding 0; permutation p for Arm A >= 0.05 (no within-repository signal beyond base rate); auc_A >= 0.60 (the classifier has signal to lose). no_group_effect requires the 90% repository-clustered interval of (auc_A - auc_A_loro) to lie inside [-0.03, +0.03] (two one-sided tests; symmetric equivalence). prefer_output requires (auc_B - auc_A_loro) >= 0.05 with the 95% interval excluding 0. prefer_output is evaluated independently and is the only result that can change what escalation keys on.

sample size and power note: minimum 300 labelled turns and at least 6 qualifying repositories, where qualifying means at least 5 positives and 5 negatives in that repository; repositories below this are kept in training but excluded from within-repository AUC and are not pooled into a synthetic group. Power is established by simulation before any label-feature relationship is examined: `power_sim.py` uses only the frozen marginal statistics (items and positives per repository, overall prevalence) and simulates intra-repository correlation from 0.05 to 0.30 with a true gap of 0.10 and a true output-feature gain of 0.05; it reports power for group_memorisation, no_group_effect and prefer_output at the frozen n. If simulated power for the 0.10 gap is below 0.70, the run still happens but the verdict is capped at `inconclusive_underpowered` and no gate mandate or belief revision follows. The earlier claim of an AUC standard error of 0.04 to 0.05 is withdrawn in favour of this simulation. Gold labels are written by a labeller who sees neither tier-1 nor tier-2 outputs and who is not the classifier author, so gold is independent of the answering models.

kill criterion: the no_group_effect verdict (equivalence within +/-0.03) ends the card with a negative result. Stop before running (not a result) if the preflight fails: fewer than 300 turns, fewer than 6 qualifying repositories, fewer than 60 positives overall, any feature derived from tier-2 output or gold, or more than 300 items missing tier-1 output.

what a pass unlocks: group_memorisation mandates repository-held-out evaluation plus the permutation test for the H5 escalation classifier only, and supplies the measurement for a B13 revision proposal; extending the mandate to admission and H10b requires each gate's own check. prefer_output, if it holds, moves escalation to tier-1 output features at no extra inference cost. A group_memorisation result without prefer_output does not change what escalation keys on.

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): S. Phase 1 is offline on cached features and outputs: 5 session-grouped folds, one LORO fold per repository, 200 permutations, the power simulation; CPU only.
