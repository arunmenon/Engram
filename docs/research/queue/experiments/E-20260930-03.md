claim: On engineering-memory questions over a PDLC project history, at a small served budget (about 300 tokens), storing raw ledger events and asking Jev one yes/no relevance question per shortlisted event is non-inferior to our current write-time LLM extraction within 3 points of answer accuracy, and it drops all gold evidence for at most 10% of questions; at a large budget (about 1,500 tokens) the comparison is reported, with extraction expected to lead. Source: N-SURVEY-20260929-01 (arXiv 2609.34227, pre-registered, code pinned by tag; paper-single-benchmark): on LoCoMo held-out conversations at about 250 served tokens, raw turns plus one Jev noul per shortlisted turn scored 77.0% against 77.5% for gpt-4o-mini fact extraction plus Jev (difference -0.5, one-sided bound -3.0 against a registered -5 margin; human strict -1.7, bound -3.9), at 0.0006 against 1.865 USD write cost per 1,000 turns and 273 ms against 816 ms read p50 versus an LLM reranker; at 20 served items extraction won (82.4 against 77.6); the rerank dropped all evidence for 10.4% of questions (22.7% of temporal, 31.4% of open-domain) and lowered correct abstention at k = 3 (54.1 against 63.6 for plain cosine). Limits that make our test necessary: chat memory, not engineering artifacts; judge, answerer, LLM reranker and extractor all gpt-4o-mini; human audit by an author, on discordant items only; closed jev-1.13.0 only. The source's name clash (its repository is called Engram) is unrelated to us. N-SURVEY-20260929-05 (checklist for System One claims) shapes the arms: repeat-call consistency reported, latency with caching off, and no combining of separate noul probabilities as a distribution.

arms (same ledger, same questions, same answerer and answer prompt, same judge; recall/no-recall gate held fixed and separate: the answerer is always called and its prompt allows "not in memory"; each arm packs units in its order until the budget is full, skipping a unit that would overflow; two predeclared budgets, S = 300 and L = 1,500 served tokens):
- A (raw floor): raw ledger events (comments, PR descriptions, design sections, ticket updates as stored), shortlist of 30 by our hybrid retriever (BM25 plus vector, RRF k = 60), packed in retriever order, no selector.
- B (decision arm): A's shortlist, one Jev noul per event with the source's wording (question "Does memory help answer query?"; true option "It states or directly implies part of the answer."; false option "It is off-topic or only shares a keyword."), packed by P(true).
- C (reported): the same noul on laya-typed-decisions served locally through Unsloth's Jev-compatible API, thresholds not used (ranking only), events over its window counted and ranked last.
- D (baseline): our current write-time LLM extraction over the same ledger (production extractor and prompt, pinned), facts retrieved by the same hybrid retriever (shortlist 30), packed in retriever order.
- D-J (reported, attribution): D's shortlist ranked by the same Jev noul, so that B minus D-J isolates the representation (raw against extracted) with the selector fixed, and D-J minus D isolates the selector on extracted facts.
- override: the card's H1 DreamBench-SWE sequences are dropped: executable-oracle agent runs on 60 three-session sequences for five arms at two budgets would need tens of millions of tokens, far beyond POLICY-v1.1's 12 runs a week; this spec measures answer accuracy on questions instead, which is the source's own endpoint. The card's label log-probability arm on an open backbone is not run here: E-20260929-03-r2 already compares open decision models with Jev on our decisions, and adding it would split power without deciding the ingestion question.

metric: answer accuracy (primary), per budget, judged against the gold short answer by a judge from a different model family than the answerer, with abstention on the 30 unanswerable questions scored correct only when the answer says the information is not in memory; evidence-dropped rate = answerable questions for which no gold evidence unit is in the packed set, per arm and budget; correct abstention rate on unanswerable questions; write cost per 1,000 events (extraction for D and D-J; embedding only for A, B, C); read p50 and p95 latency with caching off; Jev repeat flip rate for 40 questions re-scored (share of events whose rank position in the packed set changes); a blind human check of the judge on 150 answers, stratified by arm.

predeclared margin: positive at budget S needs the one-sided 95% lower bound of accuracy(B) minus accuracy(D) above minus 3 points, and the evidence-dropped rate of B on answerable questions at most 10% (point estimate). Budget L and every other contrast are reported with intervals and do not decide.

sample size and power note: 200 questions: the 120 frozen PDLC questions of PDLC-SEED-ASF plus 80 written for this spec (decision behind a design, a changed requirement, which PR implements what, what an engineer said in review), of which 30 are unanswerable from the ledger; all written, with gold answers and gold evidence units, before any arm runs. Clustering unit: the source artifact of the question's first gold evidence unit (about 60 to 70). At a B-versus-D discordance of about 0.15 on 170 answerable questions and a design effect of about 1.3, SE of the paired accuracy difference is about 3.4 points; at a true difference of 0, non-inferiority at minus 3 is shown with probability about 0.22; at a true 8-point loss, the negative rule fires with probability about 0.65. This is a pilot: it can return a clear negative for a large loss and a positive only if B is at least as good as D by a margin, and it sizes a confirmatory run (roughly 600 answerable questions for 0.8 power at a true difference of 0). The harness runs `selx power` (cluster-resampling simulation over the frozen question set, 1,000 draws, seed 20261116) before any answerer call and records it in `power.json`; the decision rule does not change with it.

kill criterion: negative if accuracy(B) minus accuracy(D) at budget S is below minus 3 points with the 95% interval wholly below zero, or B's evidence-dropped rate at S exceeds 10%. Invalid (rerun under a new spec revision, not a verdict): judge agreement with the blind human check below kappa 0.7; the judge from the same model family as the answerer; questions, answers or evidence labels edited after any arm ran; the shortlist differing between A, B and C for any question; the extraction model or prompt differing from the production pin; any question missing from an arm's output.

what a pass unlocks: the priority 1 decision at the ingestion stage: whether Engram keeps paying an LLM to extract facts from engineering history at write time, or stores raw events and lets a System One model select at read time for small-budget reads; a local measurement for B7 (lead: read-time curation beats write-time distillation) on structured engineering artifacts, where the belief is untested, and for B11 (working: a ranker must not prune; admission is separate), through the evidence-dropped and abstention rates; the attribution rows (D-J) tell whether any gain is the representation or the selector; 200 questions with gold answers and evidence for the PDLC memory benchmark (priority 4).

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): 80 new questions with gold answers and evidence units, plus evidence units for the 120 existing questions, 2 engineers x 3 days; judge check on 150 answers, 0.5 day; harness 3 engineer-days (reuses E-20260929-01-r2's PDLC-SEED-ASF loader and packer). Model tokens: write-time extraction over the ledger, about 3,000 events x about 1k = 3.0M (reused from E-20260928-11-r2's extraction of the same fixture if that run happened first with the same pinned extractor, in which case this line is 0 and the reuse is recorded); embeddings, about 0.5M; Jev: 200 questions x 30 events x about 350 tokens = 2.1M for B, 200 x 30 x about 150 = 0.9M for D-J, 40 repeats x 30 x 350 = 0.42M; Laya local, 2.1M; answerer: 200 questions x 5 arms x 2 budgets x about 0.9k average = 1.8M; judge: 2,000 answers x about 250 = 0.5M; total about 11.3M in 10 shards, aggregate hard cap 12.5M. USD: extraction about 6 USD, answerer and judge about 4 USD, Jev about 0.15 USD; aggregate cap 25 USD. Cost M.
