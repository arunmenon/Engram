claim: On our frozen PDLC memory question set and on public LoCoMo-10, our hybrid retriever's evidence-hit at k = 5 exceeds the expected hit of uniform random selection of k units from the same store by at least 15 points in every question category; and on LoCoMo, where each conversation fits the reader's window, end-to-end answer accuracy with retrieval at k = 10 is within 5 points of the full-context arm (reported, not decisive). Source: N-20261001-01 (caura-ai/caura-locomo at c55d3a3, repo-pinned, priority 4): the post's 87.27% is a no-retrieval full-context baseline (gpt-5-mini reader, GPT-4o judge, 1,344 of 1,540), not a memory score; Caura's own retrieval evidence-hit was 13.62 / 24.82 / 45.76 / 77.95% at k = 3 / 5 / 10 / 20 against 13.83 / 22.42 / 42.30 / 76.77% for random selection, and below random on multi-hop (60.28 against 67.78) and open-domain (42.39 against 47.74) at k = 10; 75 distinct ordered result sets across 1,986 questions; with abstention offered to every question, correct refusal 76.23% and false refusal 6.49%; prompt choice alone moved accuracy by 24.61 points with reader and judge fixed. Limits that make our test necessary: chat memory with about 27 sessions per conversation, one vendor deployment with candidate ids not retained, prompts tuned on the reported questions, a generous judge, no engineering artifacts. Nothing in our harness reports a retrieval number beside a random line today, and B19 (firm) records that our earlier harness leaked labels; the read-path specs on the bus all assume the base retriever beats chance.

arms (same questions per store; abstention offered on every question in every answering arm with one frozen prompt; reader and judge pinned; the reader prompt contains no benchmark content, checked by a test):
- A (retrieval): our hybrid retriever (BM25 plus vector, RRF k = 60, production config pinned) returning the top k = 3, 5, 10, 20 units; units are ledger events segmented by E-20260930-03-r2's frozen rule on PDLC and sessions on LoCoMo (Caura's unit, for comparability).
- R (random selection, no model): the expected evidence-hit of k units drawn uniformly without replacement from the question's store, computed exactly (hypergeometric) and checked against 5 seeded draws (seeds 1 to 5); store = the whole PDLC-SEED-ASF ledger for PDLC questions, the question's conversation for LoCoMo.
- F (full context, LoCoMo only): the whole conversation in the prompt; on PDLC the project ledger does not fit any pinned reader window, so F is not run there and this is stated.
- answering arms: on PDLC, A at k = 5 and k = 10 and R at k = 5 (seed 1); on LoCoMo, A at k = 10 and F only (the comparison the card asks for); the evidence-hit sweep (A and R at every k) runs on all questions with evidence on both stores and makes no model call.
- override: the card's "Arm C full context where the project fits the reader window" is limited to LoCoMo because the one frozen PDLC project is about 3M tokens; a PDLC store small enough to fit is not on the bus and is not built for this pilot.

metric: evidence-hit@k = share of answerable questions with at least one gold evidence unit in the top k (primary at k = 5), with all-evidence-hit@k (every gold unit in the top k) reported; observed minus random expectation per category with a Wilson 95% interval on the observed share (the random expectation is exact, so the interval is on the observed share only); degeneracy = number of distinct ordered top-10 result sets per store divided by its questions; answer accuracy per answering arm, scored by exact or normalised match where the gold is a short canonical answer and otherwise by a judge from a different model family than the reader, with a blind human check of 100 judged answers; correct-refusal rate on unanswerable questions and false-refusal rate on answerable ones, per arm; served tokens per question.

predeclared margin: positive needs, at k = 5 in every category of both stores, observed minus random at least 15 points (point estimate) with the Wilson lower bound above the random expectation, degeneracy at least 0.5 on each store, and false refusal of the retrieval arm at most 10% (A at k = 5 on PDLC, A at k = 10 on LoCoMo, the only retrieval arm answered there). The LoCoMo full-context comparison is reported with a paired interval and does not decide.

sample size and power note: PDLC: 170 answerable and 30 unanswerable questions in the SELX-ASF-200 categories (about 25 to 45 answerable per category). With about 3,000 or more units in the PDLC store, random expectation at k = 5 is under 1% per question, so on PDLC the test is a floor and a degeneracy screen: a category of 25 questions with a true hit of 40% has a Wilson interval of about 23% to 59%, which clears the 15-point rule. LoCoMo: all 1,982 questions with evidence in the no-model sweep (categories 1 to 4 have 96 to 841 questions; Wilson half-widths 1 to 10 points), so a small observed-minus-random gap is resolvable there; the answering arms run on 150 LoCoMo questions stratified by category (30 each in categories 1 to 4 and 30 adversarial, seed 20261019) to stay inside POLICY-v1.1. Paired accuracy differences (A at k = 10 minus F) on 120 answerable questions have SE about 3 to 4 points when clustered by conversation (10 clusters; the cluster interval is reported beside the question-level one and is too coarse to be primary, stated). No model variance enters the primary metric, which is deterministic given the frozen index.

kill criterion: negative if any category on either store has observed minus random below 5 points at k = 5, or the retrieval arm's false refusal (A5 on PDLC, A10 on LoCoMo) exceeds 10% on either store, or degeneracy is below 0.5 on either store. Invalid (rerun under a new spec revision, not a verdict): the LoCoMo file digest differing from the pin; question, gold or evidence files edited after any arm ran; the index or retriever config differing from the pin; a reader prompt containing benchmark content; the judge from the reader's model family; judge agreement with the human check below kappa 0.7; the random expectation and the mean of the 5 draws differing by more than 3 points in any category (a sampling bug).

what a pass unlocks: the control lines every read-path claim in Track B and in the PDLC memory benchmark (priority 4) is reported against, before H2, H8 or any rerank spec reports a lift; a per-category certificate that the base retriever is above chance on engineering questions, or a list of categories that need a retrieval fix first; a direct check of B19 (firm: our harness leaked labels) on the new harness through the prompt-content test; a measurement for B20 (working: headline claims narrow on a primary read) of whether our own read-path headline survives its random line.

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): no new labelling if E-20260930-03-r2's stage F0 has frozen SELX-ASF-200 (questions, gold answers, gold evidence units); otherwise this spec runs that same F0 procedure and E-20260930-03-r2 reuses it (2 engineers x 3 days, counted once); human judge check 100 answers, 0.3 day; harness 1.5 engineer-days. Model tokens: local embeddings of the PDLC ledger (about 0.5M, reused if already frozen) and LoCoMo turns (about 0.3M); reader on PDLC: 200 questions x (k5 about 1.5k, k10 about 2.5k, random k5 about 1.5k, prompt included) = about 1.1M; reader on LoCoMo: 150 questions x (k10 about 6.8k, full about 19.5k) = about 3.9M, cached tokens counted in full against the token cap; judge about 0.4M; total about 6.2M in 6 shards, aggregate hard cap 7.0M.
