claim: This is an exploratory harm and large-effect screen, not an adoption test. At the H5 write gate, with identical Jev instructions and one common, frozen acceptance rule, (1) a projection that only prunes fields and caps list lengths (B) shows no large F1 loss against raw item plus context (A) and cuts mean input tokens by at least 25%; (2) separately, a projection that adds banded labels with cut points fitted on the fitting half only (C) is compared with B under two predeclared hypotheses: superiority (C better than B) and bounded gain (C minus B under 3 F1 points). Source: N-SURVEY-20261005-01 (jev-state, repo-pinned at 3c30e89): the README reports 74.3% to 100.0% from projecting raw state into labels and states that the result is arithmetic by construction. Our earlier reading that the projection bands reuse the exact cuts and words of the label rules is unverified: bench/scenarios.ts, run.ts and results files were not fetched, so threshold identity is a hypothesis this spec does not depend on.

Relation to open work: the staleness projection spec is E-20261001-03 (the artifact staged to the reviewer identifies itself as E-20261001-03; any later -rN revision governs if one exists on the bus). It tests projection on staleness and supersession decisions and has no prune-only leakage-control arm. Different stage, different arms; not merged.

arms:
- A (baseline): raw extracted item plus its source context, restricted to the field whitelist below, H5 Jev request (Noul `quote_supports_item`, Choice `source_type`, Score `strength`), H5 instructions verbatim from the frozen template file.
- B (prune-only): same request and instructions; fields the three questions do not read are dropped (frozen keep list in `projection/keep_list.json`), lists capped at 5 by recency; no banding, no derived labels.
- C (fitted-label): B plus banded labels for three numeric fields (age in days, citation count, quote token overlap) with 3 bands each; cut points chosen on the fitting half by exhaustive search over the fitting-half deciles maximising fitting-half F1 under the common acceptance rule; cut points frozen to `fit/cutpoints.json` before the test half is read.
- Common acceptance rule (primary, all arms): accept an item iff mean P(`quote_supports_item` = yes) over the 3 reruns >= 0.5 AND the modal `source_type` is not the template's reject option (named in the frozen H5 template file). `strength` is recorded, not used for acceptance. The rule is fixed in advance and not fitted, so the A versus B contrast changes representation only.
- Pipeline comparison (secondary, reported separately, decides nothing): the same, with the 0.5 threshold replaced by a per-arm threshold fitted on the fitting half (grid 0.30 to 0.90 step 0.05, frozen before test access).

metric: on the 45 test-half items: precision, recall and F1 of accepted items against labels; mean input tokens per call; p95 latency; ECE (10 equal-width bands) with a bootstrap 95% CI. Repeats: each item is called 3 times per arm; the item-level probability is the mean of the 3 and the item-level `source_type` is the mode (ties to the reject option); the item, not the call, is the unit. F1 when an arm accepts nothing on a resample is defined as 0 and the count of such resamples is reported.

predeclared margin: B screen: no large harm if the lower 95% bound of (F1_B minus F1_A) is above -0.10 (the resolution 45 items permit), and token ratio B/A at or under 0.75 with the upper 95% bound at or under 0.80. C superiority: lower bound of (F1_C minus F1_B) above 0. C bounded gain: upper bound of (F1_C minus F1_B) below 0.03. A C result is reported as one of: superior, bounded, or neither; none of these adopts C.

sample size and power note: 90 items, split by source project and time into 45 fit / 45 test (`split.json`), at most 6 source projects. With 45 test items the paired F1 CI half-width is roughly 0.10, so this screen can detect large harm or a large gain only. A 3-point non-inferiority test needs a project-clustered power simulation on a larger label set; that is the confirmatory spec a positive screen earns, not this run. ECE on 45 items is unstable; it is reported with its interval and kills only when the whole interval is above 0.15.

kill criterion: kill B if the upper 95% bound of (F1_B minus F1_A) is below -0.03, or token ratio above 0.80. Mark C uncalibrated if the lower 95% bound of ECE_C is above 0.15.

what a pass unlocks: a confirmatory, adequately powered spec for a prune-only projection step at the write gate; local evidence on B14 (calibration of Jev's write-gate probabilities on our data with and without projection). No production change from this run.

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): 3 arms x 90 items x 3 reruns = 810 Jev calls; input capped at 1,500 tokens per call (A) and output at 150 tokens, worst case 810 x 1,650 = 1.34M tokens; estimated under 10 USD at prices pinned in the manifest. 1 people-day for the projection, fitting and leakage audit code; 0.5 for analysis.
