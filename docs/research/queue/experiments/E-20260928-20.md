claim: Replacing our hosted extractor with a self-hosted open-weight extractor of about 31B parameters lowers answer accuracy by no more than 2 points when the retrieval, answering model and judge are held fixed: tested for non-inferiority on LoCoMo (primary, powered) and screened for large harm on the PDLC seed questions. Source: N-20260928-27 (VarOps Acropolis Technical Report 01, repo b391c09, harness 93ad8b6): with gemma-4-31b-it on one H200 building the memory record and gemini-3.7-flash reading, scores were within 1 to 2 points of all-frontier (LongMemEval-S 89.6 vs 91.4, LoCoMo 81.8 vs 81.8, BEAM 100K 55.2 vs 56.2); local loss sat in the reading seats and grew with length (2.2, 5.5, 8.0 points). Caveats that make our test necessary: the frontier model was also the judge for every row; placement rows were single runs against the report's own noise floor of about 1 point overall; "own everything" was the better of two local configurations; the local-versus-frontier split was measured on BEAM 100K only; all three datasets are conversational; a vendor evaluated its own product and no third party has re-run it.

arms (one frozen memory pipeline; same corpus, retrieval, served-token budget, answering prompt and judge; only the extractor, and in arm C also the reader, differs):
- A: our current hosted extractor (Engram production extractor, pinned model and prompt) with the pinned hosted reader.
- A2: a second, independent build with A's settings, to measure extraction run-to-run variation (the noise floor for B against A).
- B: gemma-4-31b-it (pinned revision) self-hosted on one GPU via vLLM as the extractor, with the same extraction prompt and output schema, and the pinned hosted reader.
- C: all-local, reported, not decided on: B's extractor and gemma-4-31b-it as reader, to price the answer seat (the source's "own everything" row, without best-of-two selection).

metric: answer accuracy (primary), graded by a judge from a model family different from every reader and extractor (not Gemini, not Gemma, not the hosted extractor's or reader's family), with a 10% human-checked subsample for judge agreement; paired per-question differences with McNemar's test; answerable-only accuracy and abstention on unanswerable questions reported separately (refusal posture moves points between the two, per the source); extraction cost per 1,000 writes, GPU hours and extraction latency.

predeclared margin: B is non-inferior if the lower bound of the paired 95% interval of (B minus A) accuracy on LoCoMo is above minus 2 points (the card's margin); PDLC harm screen: B's PDLC accuracy not more than 5 points below A's (point estimate). override: the card judged the 2-point kill on our PDLC memory questions. That set does not exist yet (PC-SCOPE-20260928-01 was rejected) and the PDLC seed carries 120 questions, where the paired SE is about 2.5 to 3 points, so a 2-point rule there would decide on noise; the powered non-inferiority test moves to LoCoMo, and the PDLC set screens for a large loss only. LongMemEval-S, also named on the card, is not used: extraction over its 500 haystacks is about 57M input tokens per arm, about 29 runs per arm under the 2M-token cap.

sample size and power note: LoCoMo: all questions in categories 1 to 4 (about 1,540 answerable), adversarial questions reported separately. At a paired discordance rate of about 0.10 the SE of the accuracy difference is sqrt(0.10 / 1540) = 0.81 points, so the lower 95% bound is the observed difference minus 1.6 points; if the extractors are truly equal, the bound clears minus 2 about 80% of the time. LoCoMo has only 10 conversations, so a conversation-cluster bootstrap is too coarse; the paired question-level interval is primary and the conversation-cluster interval is reported beside it, and A versus A2 shows how much of any gap is extraction noise. PDLC: 120 questions, one build per arm, reported with question-level intervals.

kill criterion: point estimate of (B minus A) on LoCoMo below minus 2 points, or B more than 5 points below A on PDLC questions: negative. Invalid (rerun under a new spec revision, not a verdict): judge agreement with the human subsample below 0.85, A versus A2 disagreeing by more than 2 points overall (the pipeline is too noisy to decide a 2-point question), or any served-token budget differing between arms.

what a pass unlocks: the priority 2 goal at the ingestion stage: memory text stays in-house at write time with only the answer step sent to a hosted model; an independent check of the vendor report's extractor-seat result; a measured price of the answer seat (arm C) for any later all-local plan.

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): extraction: LoCoMo (10 conversations, about 0.3M input tokens per build before prompt overhead) and PDLC seed (about 0.5M), for A, A2 and B, about 3M tokens, 2 runs (B and C self-hosted on one GPU); answering and judging: LoCoMo about 1,540 questions x 4 arms x about 3.5k tokens, about 21.6M tokens, 11 runs; PDLC 120 questions x 4 arms, about 1.7M tokens, 1 run. About 14 runs, under 80 USD including GPU rent. Harness 4 engineer-days (the PDLC questions are shared with E-20260928-18). Cost M.
