claim: Inside one otherwise identical system, writing supersedes and contradicts edges at write time (superseded items kept but excluded from recall, contradicts edges inhibited at read time) raises knowledge-update accuracy by at least 5 points over the same system with every new unit simply added, with no loss beyond 2 points on other question classes.

arms:
- Arm A (edges off): "prefer add over modify" kept; every new unit added; no supersedes or contradicts edges; recall unchanged.
- Arm B (edges on, two-signal rule): supersede only when contradiction >= 0.7 AND a named target id is returned (jevmem's rule); the old item is kept, tagged superseded (P5 `superseded` state) and excluded from recall; contradicts edges are dropped from expansion at read time (REALM's conflict_inhibit).
- Arm B2 (single-signal rule): as Arm B but supersede on contradiction >= 0.7 alone, targeting the top candidate.
- Everything else fixed: extractor, embedder, fusion, k, token budget, answerer and judge. One ingestion pass: the extractor and the per-unit contradiction decision (score and target id over up to 10 candidates) run once and are logged; Arms A, B and B2 are produced by applying their rules to the same logged decisions, so the arms differ only in the rule.

metric: primary: knowledge-update accuracy (frozen judge), pooled over LongMemEval_S knowledge-update questions and PDLC knowledge-update questions. Secondary: accuracy on the 100-question sample of other LongMemEval classes; false-supersede rate on near-misses and missed-supersede rate on reversals in the contradiction dev set; served tokens.

predeclared margin: edges_help requires B minus A >= 0.05 on pooled knowledge-update accuracy with the 95% cluster-bootstrap interval excluding 0, AND the lower bound of B minus A on other classes > -0.02, AND B's false supersedes on near-misses <= 1 of at least 60 (the card's "1 in 16 equivalent" made concrete; the one-sided 95% upper bound is reported). two_signal_needed: B2's false supersedes exceed B's by at least 3 near-misses.

sample size and power note: LongMemEval_S has 78 knowledge-update questions (per N-CAT-13's per-type counts); alone they detect only about a 12-point difference at 80% power with 15% discordance. The PDLC set adds at least 200 knowledge-update questions from at least 8 of our repositories, for about 280 in total, where the detectable difference is about 6.5 points; a true 5-point effect will often land as inconclusive, and the spec says so rather than lowering the margin. The contradiction dev set has at least 40 reversals and at least 60 near-misses (jevmem's set had 27 and 16), labelled by someone other than the rule author. Clusters: LongMemEval question haystack, and repository for PDLC items.

kill criterion: edges_bounded: the 95% upper bound of B minus A on pooled knowledge-update accuracy is below 0.05. Separately, two_signal_unneeded if B2's false supersedes are no more than B's (the two-signal rule is unnecessary). Stop before running (not a result) if P5's `superseded` state is not implemented, H2's harness is not frozen, or any pin is missing.
