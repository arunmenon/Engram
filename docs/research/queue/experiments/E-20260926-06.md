claim: On our corpus, one listwise decision-model choice call over the fused pool beats the best local reranker (MiniLM cross-encoder or self-hosted bge-reranker-v2-m3) by at least 3 points of end answer accuracy at 5 served memories, not only on recall@1.

arms:
- Arm 0 (reference): RRF fusion order, no rerank.
- Arm A: one listwise choice call over the whole pool; rank positions are the scores (Hindsight design).
- Arm B: one yes/no call per candidate, batched into one request, sorted by probability (the pointwise design Hindsight measured against). Run on the 30-candidate pool only.
- Arm C: local cross-encoder ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2 (Hindsight's default and Hippo's fallback).
- Arm D: self-hosted bge-reranker-v2-m3.
- Shared: identical pool per query, candidate text truncated to 120 tokens for every arm (keeps the 240 pool under Jev's 32,000-token context), identical seeded candidate order; order control on a 100-query subset with two further orders for Arm A; same frozen answerer and judge; served sets of 2 and 5 memories.

metric: primary: end answer accuracy by the frozen judge at 5 served memories on the 30-candidate pool. Secondary: accuracy at 2 served memories; the 240 pool (Arms 0, A and best local only, on a 200-query subset); recall@1 and recall@5 against gold evidence; decision-model tokens and cost per query; p50 and p95 latency.

predeclared margin: hosted_wins requires A minus max(C, D) >= 0.03 at 5 memories with the Holm-adjusted 95% cluster-bootstrap lower bound > 0 (Holm over the two primary comparisons A minus C and A minus D). B10 replication (secondary): A minus B recall@1 >= 0 with the 95% interval lower bound > -0.02.

sample size and power note: Hippo's one answer-level test (n = 150, 98.33% intervals) was a tie at 5 memories (Jev minus CE -0.0067) with a gain only at 2 memories (base 0.0867, CE 0.1067, Jev 0.1400). For paired binary answers with about 10% discordance, a 3-point difference needs about 870 queries for 80% power; at 600 queries the detectable difference is about 3.5 points. Target at least 600 answerable P3 queries plus the 100 P0 queries, clustered by conversation or project (at least 30 clusters). Because the burden of proof sits on the hosted call (egress and cost), an underpowered result defaults to local.

kill criterion: hosted_bounded: the 95% upper bound of A minus max(C, D) at 5 memories is below 0.03 (the hosted call's only remaining benefit would be a shorter context, which does not justify sending memory text to a third party).
