claim: With the store, ingestion, supersession decisions, retrieval, served-token budget, reader and the validity metadata on current items all held fixed, additionally serving the superseded values that retrieval returns, each with its validity dates and supersedes link and packed after every current item (supersede-and-retain, current first), raises previous-value accuracy by at least 15 points over serving only current items (supersede-and-hide) while costing no more than 3 points of current-value accuracy as a point estimate; this is a pilot, and the current-value side is a screen, not a non-inferiority test. The estimand is named: the effect of retaining dated superseded items in the served context under current-first packing, with metadata matched on current items; it is not the effect of an on-demand history call, which is not tested. Source: N-20260929-20 (MemProbe, arXiv 2609.30558, EMNLP 2026, MIT code and data pinned at 9068d8d): under one shared reader and typed-field scoring with no LLM judge, Graphiti and LangMem tie overall (62.5 against 65.2, paired bootstrap p = 0.534) but differ on previous-value 96.4 against 67.9 (p = 0.002, surviving Bonferroni over 18), while Graphiti trails on current-value 66.1 against 80.4 (p = 0.041, not Bonferroni-significant); the challenger checked these against Table 1 and section 5.2 (REV-20260930-07). Limits that make our test necessary: a whole-system comparison of two products, not a serving-policy toggle; Graphiti's adapter returned top-15 edge facts without validity timestamps, so old and new facts sat side by side undated; 28 previous-value probes per system; one run per system; about 16% of Graphiti session-adds lost to malformed extraction JSON; synthetic chat episodes with atomic facts. Merged card PC-20260930-04 (N-20260930-13, KIP 2.0 at 69a05ff, repo-pinned, no accuracy numbers) adds the design point that "the world changed" and "we were wrong" are different histories: a world change keeps the old value true for its period, a correction removes it for all times. This spec labels each PDLC change episode with its change kind and reports the retain-minus-hide difference per kind, so a retention policy that misleads on corrections is visible; it does not build a typed change arm (no effect size exists to justify one yet).

arms (one store instance per episode, built once and shared by every arm: same ingestion through our write path, same supersession decisions from our current linker recorded in the P5 `superseded` state, same hybrid retrieval with the same served-token budget of 2,000 tokens, same reader and reader prompt; in every arm each served current item is rendered with `valid_from` (its `recorded_at`) and `valid_to: current`, so metadata on current items is identical across arms):
- A (supersede-and-hide, control): superseded items are excluded; current items are served in retrieval order with the matched metadata.
- B (supersede-and-retain, current first, decision arm): all current items that retrieval returns are packed first in retrieval order, then the superseded items that retrieval returns, in retrieval order, each rendered with `valid_from`, `valid_to` (the superseding item's `recorded_at`) and `superseded_by: <id>`, until the budget is full; this is the exact packing rule a pass would adopt.
- B-u (reported, presentation control): superseded items retained but rendered like current items (no `valid_to`, no `superseded_by`) and interleaved in retrieval order, the undated side-by-side condition of the source's Graphiti row; B minus B-u shows how much of any effect is the dating and ordering rather than the retention.
- R (reported, no decision): MemProbe's full-context reader baseline on suite56, to place the arms against the source's reference row (88.8 in the paper).
- override: the card's "3 runs per arm" becomes 2 reader repeats per arm for the three served arms, to fit the added control inside the same budget; ingestion runs once per episode and is shared, because repeating it would change the store between arms and make the serving rule unattributable.

metric: per arm, accuracy on current-value, previous-value and change-detection probes, each scored by MemProbe's `score_answer()` typed-field comparison against gold (no LLM judge); reported beside them: overall accuracy, MemProbe's Plasticity, Stability and SP-Balance, source and conflict-value probes, served tokens, the share of probes whose served set contains at least one superseded item (only these can differ between A and B on current-value), the share of probes where the superseding link is missing (our linker missed the update), and on the 30 PDLC episodes, B minus A per change kind (world_change, correction) on the as-of-date probes, descriptive.

predeclared margin: pilot pass needs (B minus A) previous-value accuracy at least 15 points with the two-sided 95% interval excluding zero, and (B minus A) current-value accuracy above minus 3 points as a point estimate. The current-value one-sided 95% lower bound is reported and does not decide; no default changes on this run.

sample size and power note: previous-value probes: 28 in suite56 plus 30 in the PDLC episodes (one per episode), 58 in total; current-value probes: the suite56 count, read from the pinned repository at stage 0 and recorded in `power.json` before any model call, plus 30 PDLC. Clustering unit: episode (56 plus 30 = 86). Previous-value: discordance between arms expected 0.3 to 0.5; SE of the paired difference about 9 points; a true 30-point gain passes the 15-point rule with probability about 0.95, a true 20-point gain about 0.7. Current-value: discordance about 0.08 on roughly 60 to 90 probes, SE about 3 to 3.5 points; a true 0 difference fires the minus 3 point-estimate kill with probability about 0.2. The confirmatory current-value non-inferiority test at a 3-point margin, one-sided alpha 0.05 and power 0.8 at a true difference of 0, needs about (1.645 plus 0.842) squared x 0.08 / 0.03 squared x 1.2, about 660 current-value probes whose served set contains a superseded item; `power.json` records this figure, and the run recomputes it from the observed paired variance in `metrics.csv`. The per-kind PDLC rows (about 15 episodes each) are descriptive only.

kill criterion: negative if (B minus A) current-value accuracy is below minus 3 points (point estimate), or (B minus A) previous-value accuracy is below 5 points (point estimate). Invalid (rerun under a new spec revision, not a verdict): the served store differing between arms for any episode (per-episode store sha256); current-item metadata rendering differing between A, B and B-u (rendering sha256 per probe); served tokens over budget in any probe; the reader, reader prompt or MemProbe scoring code differing from the pin; gold or change-kind labels of the PDLC episodes edited after any arm ran; fewer than 25 PDLC episodes passing the authoring toolkit's validation; fewer than 8 episodes of either change kind; any probe missing from A or B without an explicit failed record.

what a pass unlocks: a funded confirmatory run, not a default change: the measured previous-value gain and the current-value paired variance that size a non-inferiority test for the read-surface policy for supersession (R2), where engineering agents need both "what is the requirement now" and "what did we decide before"; a scoring rule for the PDLC memory benchmark (priority 4) that reports current-value, previous-value and change-detection separately; per-kind rows that tell whether the correction-versus-world-change split (merged card PC-20260930-04) is worth its own experiment; a local measurement beside B3 (lead): this spec holds the supersede edges fixed and tests how they are served, so it narrows what B3's knowledge-update gain can depend on; the missing-link share tells E-20260926-07-r2 how often our linker fails to record an update.

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): 30 PDLC change episodes authored by T1 with MemProbe's toolkit (a requirement, design decision or constraint changes mid-history; at least 10 world changes and 10 corrections, the kind labelled by the author and checked by a second engineer; current, previous and change-detection probes with gold; for correction episodes the as-of-date probe's gold is the corrected value, since the earlier statement was wrong at every time), 3.5 engineer-days, second engineer validates gold and kind, 1 day; adapter for our store (MemProbe's `reset`, `ingest`, `retrieve`) 2 engineer-days. Model tokens: ingestion through our extraction model, about 1,700 sessions x about 1.6k = 2.7M; reader: about 314 probes x 3 arms x 2 repeats x about 2.4k = 4.5M, plus R on suite56 once, about 0.2M; total about 7.4M in 7 shards. USD: extraction model about 5 USD, reader about 3 USD; aggregate cap 20 USD. Cost S.
