claim: For the save/skip write-admission gate and the served-memory injection gate, isotonic recalibration fitted per source (chat turns, repo docs, tool output) with cross-fitting over whole sessions or documents lowers the held-out Brier score relative to raw probabilities, with the cluster-bootstrap interval excluding zero, and lowers held-out ECE by at least 2x, on at least 2 of 3 sources for each gate. Selection precision at matched coverage is reported as a check, not claimed: within a source a monotone map preserves ranking, so it is expected not to change.

arms:
- Arm A: raw decision-model probabilities; for selection, the fixed production threshold 0.5 for both gates.
- Arm A2: raw probabilities with one threshold fitted on the fitting half, pooled across sources, targeting 80% coverage.
- Arm A3 (added control): raw probabilities with a threshold fitted per source on the fitting half at the same 80% coverage target and the same tie rule as Arm B. This separates source-specific thresholding from recalibration.
- Arm B: isotonic recalibration fitted per source on the fitting half; for selection, a per-source threshold at the same coverage target and tie rule.
- Arm B2 (secondary): Platt scaling with the same protocol.
- Coverage means, for the admission gate, the fraction of items saved; for the injection gate, the fraction of lines served (not blocked).

metric: calibration endpoint (primary): held-out Brier score and held-out log loss per source and gate (proper scoring rules), plus ECE with 10 equal-mass bins and calibration slope as corroboration. Selection endpoint (secondary, separate): precision at matched achieved coverage (each arm's threshold set at the scoring half's own 80% quantile, which isolates ranking) and precision at transferred threshold (fitting-half threshold applied to the scoring half, reporting the achieved coverage per arm). Gate-level precision is the unweighted mean over the three sources.

predeclared margin: pass on a gate if, on at least 2 of 3 sources, Brier(A) - Brier(B) > 0 with the 95% cluster-bootstrap interval excluding 0 AND ECE(A) / max(ECE(B), 0.005) >= 2. Pass overall requires both gates to pass. Selection check: B minus A3 precision at matched achieved coverage is expected within +/-1 point; a larger difference is reported as a tie-handling or threshold-transfer effect and is not credited to recalibration.

sample size and power note: at least 150 labelled items per source per gate (900 total) and at least 20 positives and 20 negatives per source per gate, so a 75-item scoring half has about 7 to 8 items per ECE bin (10 bins instead of 15, following the source's exclusion of per-variable fits below 20 positives). Items are clustered (turns of a session, chunks of a document, lines of a tool trace); the effective n is the number of clusters, recorded in the manifest. Power comes from a pre-registered simulation (`power_sim.py`) that uses only the frozen marginals (items, clusters and prevalence per source and gate) and plausible raw miscalibration levels taken from the source's Table 4 range (ECE 0.012 to 0.046, slope 0.51 to 1.24), and reports power for the Brier interval excluding 0 and for the 2x ECE ratio at the frozen n. If simulated power is below 0.70 for a gate, that gate's verdict is capped at inconclusive. B21 is not used as a power argument; it sizes rare-event bounds only.

kill criterion: for both gates, on all three sources, the 95% interval of Brier(A) - Brier(B) includes 0 and ECE ratio < 1.3. Stop before running (not a result) if any source and gate has fewer than 150 items, fewer than 20 positives or 20 negatives, or items without a cluster id.

what a pass unlocks: a per-source recalibration step in the recipe for these two gates, which makes probability thresholds and borderline bands interpretable across sources (the H5 cascade escalates on a [0.3, 0.7] band, which assumes calibrated tiers, hence B13). It does not by itself establish a requirement for later gates; each gets its own check. Moves B14 only by what the data show.

No model calls: probabilities are logged in the H5 and H10b shadow runs; the preflight refuses the run if any labelled item lacks a logged probability, rather than calling the gate. Compute: 20 cross-fit repeats x 2 halves x 5 arms x 3 sources x 2 gates on CPU, under 1 wall-clock hour. Labels dominate: up to 900 items, about 4 to 6 people-days if H5 and H10b labels are not already split by source, plus about 1 people-day for analysis. Proposed cap for the desk: 0 model tokens, 2 wall-clock hours of compute, 7 people-days. Budget: policy unset.

evidence scope: this spec rests only on N-20260926-07 (arxiv 2609.24052: pooled raw ECE 0.0231 vs 0.0069 after isotonic or Platt recalibration, a factor of 3.3, with narrative-level cross-fitting; Table 4 per-variable ECE 0.0122 to 0.0455 and slopes 0.51 to 1.24; section 4.2 notes that monotone recalibration preserves ranking).
