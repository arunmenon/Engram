claim: For implements links from a requirement or design section to the pull requests that deliver it, ranking each requirement's candidate pool (merged PRs within 180 days, capped at the 40 nearest in time) with a LambdaMART model over text similarity plus repository metadata (time proximity, file overlap with paths named in the requirement or design, diff size) and asking Jev only about the top 5, at the same decision threshold, is non-inferior within 3 F1 points to asking Jev about every candidate, at no more than one fifth of the model calls; the ranker alone is reported against both. Sources: N-SURVEY-20260929-06 (paper-ablated): LinkRank (arXiv 2607.23610), a LightGBM LambdaMART over 17 features on 3,103 issues and 7,688 commits in six large projects, reached average F1 73.17 against 48.39 for a GPT-OSS-120B agent (LinkAnchor) and 35.88 for SBERT plus GPT-4o rerank, in 19.6 against 421 minutes of test time; these three figures are from Table 4, the known-K evaluation, which uses the true number of links per issue (an oracle cut-off); the unknown-K comparison in Table 5 is the deployment-relevant setting and its values are not in our note, so no Table 5 number is quoted here and the operational case rests on this spec's own measurement; removing metadata caused the largest drop (PyTorch: all features 76.38, SBERT only 44.69, metadata only 38.73). For requirement-to-code links, arXiv 2509.05585 section 4.4.1, as checked by the challenger (REV-20260930-10), reports the "highest average F1-score of 0.7219" for HGT-All against "Gemini-None (average F1 = 0.6535)"; the pair 0.7155 against 0.5874 quoted in E-20260930-04 from N-SURVEY-20260929-06 could not be matched to one table and aggregation and is withdrawn. N-SURVEY-20260929-04: Jev had the highest contradiction recall of ten models on ContractNLI at a fraction of the cost, which is why Jev is the decider held constant here. Limits that make our test necessary: LinkRank's gold links are developer references the authors call inconsistent; issue-to-commit in large Apache and PyTorch projects, not requirement-to-PR in spec-driven repositories; the abstract and tables disagree (74.54 against 73.17); its headline uses oracle K; no paper puts a System One model behind a ranker.

arms (same test requirements, same capped candidate pools, same rendered (requirement, PR) pair for every model):
- B (baseline): Jev noul "Does this pull request implement this requirement?" (true and false criteria fixed in `prompts/implements.json`) on every candidate in the pool; link if P(true) >= t_B, t_B fitted on dev.
- C (decision arm): A's top 5, then the same Jev noul, linking at the same t_B; C's decisions are therefore exactly B's decisions restricted to A's top 5 (C reads B's cached answers; no new calls).
- C-fit (reported): as C, with its own threshold t_C fitted on dev.
- A (reported, no model): LambdaMART over BM25, TF-IDF cosine, embedding cosine (pinned local embedder), time proximity, file overlap with paths named in the requirement or design, diff size, and the other LinkRank metadata features that SpecMine records; link if normalised score >= t_A (LinkRank's absolute stop, fitted on dev).
- D (reported): A's top 5, then a single-call LLM checker (the checker pinned in E-20260928-08-r2, same prompt); link on its verdict.
- E (reported, co-primary for the kill only): A's top 5, then Winnow-12B Q8 through the same request contract as E-20260929-03-r2's W arm; link if P(true) >= t_E fitted on dev.
- override: the card's pool of plus or minus 180 days is kept, capped at the 40 PRs nearest in time when larger, applied identically to every arm; the share of gold PRs outside the cap is reported. The card's internal repository is dropped: POLICY-v1.1 names no dogfood tenant.

metric: link F1 on test requirements at dev-fit thresholds (primary), micro-averaged over (requirement, PR) pairs; precision at recall 0.8 (threshold-free, reported); top-5 retention = share of test requirements with at least one gold PR whose gold PRs are all within A's top 5, computed separately on the pooled labels and on the exhaustively labelled subset; estimated unjudged-gold rate from the random audit outside the pool, with its interval, and the F1 of B and C recomputed under the audit-corrected labels as a sensitivity row; model calls, USD and p95 latency per 1,000 requirements; share of gold PRs outside the 40-PR cap.

predeclared margin: positive needs the one-sided 95% lower bound of F1(C) minus F1(B) above minus 3 points, top-5 retention at least 0.90 (point estimate) on both the pooled labels and the exhaustive subset, and C's model calls at most one fifth of B's (recorded, not assumed). Wilson intervals on both retention estimates are reported; with 24 exhaustively labelled requirements the subset interval is wide (about plus or minus 12 points) and is stated as such.

sample size and power note: 120 test requirements from at least 4 held-out repositories, about 1 to 3 gold PRs each, pools of up to 40. Because C now uses t_B, C differs from B only on (i) gold PRs that B links outside A's top 5 (C misses them) and (ii) B's false positives outside the top 5 (C avoids them); the paired F1 difference is a deterministic function of B's cached answers and A's ranking, so its uncertainty is the resampling of requirements alone. With about 250 pairs linked by either arm, an expected discordance of about 0.06 and a design effect of about 1.5 (clustering by requirement), SE of the F1 difference is about 2 points; at a true difference of 0, non-inferiority at minus 3 is shown with probability about 0.6; at a true 6-point loss, the negative rule fires with probability about 0.75. `lr power` (cluster-resampling simulation over the dev labels applying the shared-threshold rule exactly, 1,000 draws, seed 20261102) runs after the dev labels freeze and before any test-set threshold use, and is recorded in `power.json`; the decision rule does not change with it. Top-5 retention: 120 requirements give a Wilson interval of about plus or minus 5 points around 0.90 on the pooled labels.

kill criterion: negative if both C and E are more than 3 F1 points below B with each interval wholly below zero, or top-5 retention is below 0.90 (point estimate) on either label set. Invalid (rerun under a new spec revision, not a verdict): labeller kappa on implements below 0.6; any test repository used in ranker training or threshold fitting; the rendered pair differing between model arms; pools differing between arms; the Jev version or noul text differing from the pin; fewer than 100 test requirements with at least one gold PR; fewer than 20 exhaustively labelled requirements with at least one gold PR; labels written after their freeze; any declared arm without an output or an explicit failed record for every pair it covers.

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): labelling: pooled candidates for 120 test and 30 dev requirements (union of A's top 10, B's top 10 by P(true) and every developer-referenced PR, about 12 per requirement, about 1,800 pairs, about 25 labeller-hours); exhaustive labelling of every pool candidate for 24 test requirements drawn at random (seed 20261102), about 960 pairs, about 16 labeller-hours; a random audit of 3 pool candidates outside the labelling pool for each of the other 96 test requirements, 288 pairs, about 5 labeller-hours; two labellers, adjudicated; ranker training labels are developer-referenced links in the training repositories (silver, no labelling); harness and feature extraction 4 engineer-days.
