claim: On the P0 corpus, as later events are appended after a snapshot position S (+25%, +50%, +100% items), BM25 with live corpus statistics fails to reproduce the snapshot-time top-10 over items that existed at S and did not change, on at least one query at the first growth step, while BM25 with statistics frozen at S reproduces it exactly at every step; and for the vector channel, an as-of search that filters after the ANN search loses as-of recall@10 against an exact as-of reference, while pre-filtering or a per-snapshot index does not.

arms (same corpus, same 200 queries, same tokeniser and embedder unless the axis says otherwise; every arm is compared with the ranking computed at S):
- Arm A: BM25 with live corpus statistics at each growth step, results restricted to items valid at S.
- Arm B: BM25 with df, N and avgdl recomputed as of S (per-document tf and length rows with validity intervals, as in Staudinger et al.).
- Arm C: as-of vector search, HNSW over all versions, over-fetch 10x k, then filter to items valid at S.
- Arm D1: as-of vector search with the time filter applied inside the ANN search (filtered HNSW on the grown index).
- Arm D2: per-snapshot vector index built at S and never mutated.
- Reference: exact (brute-force) cosine kNN over items valid at S with the pinned embedder; this is the as-of ground truth for C, D1 and D2.
- Embedding axis (applied to D2 and the reference): pinned embedder version vs a second declared embedder version standing in for an unpinned upgrade; the second version is named in pins, not chosen after results.

metric: primary: exact reproduction rate per arm and growth step (share of queries whose top-10 id list, restricted to items valid and unchanged at S, equals the list at S). Secondary: set equality at 10; Kendall tau over the union of the two lists; as-of recall@10 of C, D1 and D2 against the exact reference; latency p50 and p95 per query; storage overhead relative to the live index; for the embedding axis, reproduction rate across the two embedder versions.

predeclared margin: this is a contract fixture under P0's zero-violation rule, not a statistical claim. freeze_scoring_inputs if repro_A < 1.0 at any growth step and repro_B == 1.0 at every step. Vector: post_filter_dilutes if recall_C < recall_D_best at any step, where recall_D_best is the higher of D1 and D2; exact_as_of_vector requires the chosen D arm to have recall 1.0 against the reference (identical to exact kNN) at every step, or the manifest must pin an index per snapshot. Embedder pinning is required if reproduction across embedder versions is below 1.0.

sample size and power note: 200 queries (120 drawn from P0 Spec retrieval logs, 80 written against the corpus at S before any growth step is computed), 3 growth steps. Outcomes are exact counts, reported with Clopper-Pearson intervals; clustering unit for any rate interval is the Spec that the query came from (queries written against the corpus form their own cluster per author). A single non-reproduced query is sufficient to fail an exactness contract, so power is not the constraint; the intervals size the drift for the latency and storage trade-off.
