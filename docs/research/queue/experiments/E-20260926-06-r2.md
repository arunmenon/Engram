claim: On our corpus, one listwise decision-model choice call over the fused pool beats the local reranker chosen on a dev split (MiniLM cross-encoder or self-hosted bge-reranker-v2-m3) by at least 3 points of end answer accuracy at 5 served memories on the test split, not only on recall@1. Separately, and without revising B10's LoCoMo result: whether per-candidate yes/no questions in one request (Arm B) rank relevant evidence higher than the single choice (Arm A) when several candidates are relevant (merged from PC-20260927-03).

arms:
- Arm 0 (reference): RRF fusion order, no rerank.
- Arm A: one listwise choice call over the whole pool; rank positions are the scores (Hindsight design, B10's shape).
- Arm B: one yes/no question per candidate, all in a single request, sorted by probability (the hev/reranker "Noul batch" shape). This is NOT the separate-call pointwise design B10 measured against; the call-count comparison in B10 is not tested here. Pool 30 only.
- Arm C: local cross-encoder ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2.
- Arm D: self-hosted bge-reranker-v2-m3.
- Arm L*: whichever of C and D has higher accuracy at 5 memories on the dev split; chosen once, written to `selection.json` and frozen before any test query is scored. The unselected local arm is not run on test.
- Shared: identical pool per query, candidate text truncated to 120 tokens for every arm (keeps the 240 pool under the decision model's 32,000-token context), identical seeded candidate order; order control on a 100-query test subset with two further orders for Arm A; the answerer and the judge are pinned models from different families from each other and from the decision model; the judge sees only (question, answer, reference) with arm labels stripped.

dispatch (exact; nothing else is run):
- dev (150 queries): pool 30; arms C, D; served 5; answerer and judge.
- test pool 30 (700 queries: 600 P3 + 100 P0): arms 0, A, B, L*; served 2 and 5; answerer and judge. Rankings for A and B also scored for nDCG@10 and recall@k.
- test pool 240 (fixed seeded 200-query subset of the 600 P3): arms 0, A, L*; served 5; answerer and judge.
- order control (fixed seeded 100-query subset): Arm A only, pool 30, two extra orders, rankings only.

metric: primary: end answer accuracy by the blinded judge at 5 served memories on test pool 30, Arm A vs L*. Secondary: accuracy at 2 served memories; pool 240 accuracy; recall@1 and recall@5 against gold evidence; decision-model tokens and cost per query; p50 and p95 latency. Separate result (from PC-20260927-03): nDCG@10 and gold recall@5 for Arm B vs Arm A on test pool 30, split by number of gold-relevant items (exactly 1 vs at least 3).

predeclared margin: hosted_wins: (acc5_A - acc5_L*) >= 0.03 with the 95% cluster-bootstrap lower bound > 0. Because L* is selected on dev and only one hosted-vs-local comparison is made on test, no selection or multiplicity adjustment is needed. multi_relevant_B_better: on the at-least-3 split, (ndcg_B - ndcg_A) >= 0.02 with the 95% interval excluding 0, AND on the exactly-1 split ci95_low(ndcg_B - ndcg_A) >= -0.01 is reported (the card's non-inferiority condition).

The burden of proof sits on the hosted call (egress and cost), so an underpowered result defaults to local. Minimum counts: 600 P3 and 100 P0 test queries, at least 30 clusters; for the merged split, at least 150 test queries with at least 3 gold items and at least 150 with exactly 1 (P0 build-time reads are expected to supply most multi-relevant queries); if either split falls short, that result is reported as descriptive only.
