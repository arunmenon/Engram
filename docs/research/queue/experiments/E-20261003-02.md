claim: On our seeded project history with read receipts and produced-artifact links recorded, receipt-graph rollback with selective replay recovers at least 25 points more fault cases than deleting only the bad memory, while keeping at least 95% of benign memories (full reset keeps 0%), both under oracle diagnosis and under the H10a staleness detector's diagnosis.

Sources. N-SURVEY-20261002-06 (2608.10502): controlled n=150, GPT-4o, recovery 0.853 vs full reset 0.407 and delete-retrieved 0.340; benign preservation 1.000 vs 0.000; recurrence 0.266, worse than LLM-judge 0.121; ablation without selective replay 0.840 recovery, 0.071 recurrence at 24.01 calls; transfer n=50 0.680 vs 0.540. Limits: oracle diagnosis only, single run, no intervals, authors call controlled results an upper bound. The card matches the numbers table. No register experiment or open spec tests recovery (R8 lists it); H10 detects stale items but never repairs, so no merge.

arms (same fault cases, same pinned agent model, same seeded history and receipts):
- A (baseline): delete the source bad memory only.
- B (reported floor on benign retention): full reset of the agent's memory for the project.
- C (decision arm): receipt-graph rollback: from the bad memory, follow read receipts to sessions that read it and their produced memories and artifacts; quarantine affected derived memories unless independently supported by an unaffected source (support check, affected nodes cannot vouch for each other, B25); selectively replay affected sessions.
- diagnosis factor: each arm under oracle diagnosis and under H10a detector diagnosis (the detector's flags at its frozen threshold).

fault cases: 60 (20 poisoned, 20 stale, 20 wrong-scope), each injected at a seeded point in the history and followed by 3 to 5 sessions that read it.

metric: recovery = re-run of the probe task after repair produces the correct artifact (verifier); benign retention = share of pre-fault benign memories still present and unchanged; recurrence = fault value reappears in the next 5 sessions (over recovered cases); LLM calls and tokens per case.

predeclared margin: positive needs, under oracle diagnosis, recovery(C) minus recovery(A) at least 0.25 with the 95% cluster-bootstrap lower bound above 0.10, benign retention(C) at least 0.95, and under detector diagnosis recovery(C) minus recovery(A) at least 0.15 (point estimate). Negative: oracle recovery(C) minus recovery(A) below 0.10.

sample size and power note: 60 cases, clustered by injection point in the history (about 20 clusters). With true recovery 0.8 vs 0.35 (note's ratio, discounted), the clustered SE of the paired difference is about 0.08, so the 0.10 lower bound clears with probability about 0.95 at the note's effect and about 0.6 at a true 25-point gain. This is a pilot: recurrence is reported with intervals, not decided.

kill criterion: negative if oracle recovery(C) minus recovery(A) is below 0.10; kill C regardless if benign retention(C) is below 0.90 or C's mean LLM calls per case exceeds 10x A's. Invalid: receipts missing for more than 5% of sessions; fault cases edited after any arm ran; verifier changed after freeze; fewer than 54 scored cases; pins differ; detector threshold not the frozen H10a value.

what a pass unlocks: the P4/P5 field decision (record memory ids read and artifacts produced per session) while they are additive and cheap, before Consumer 4 ships; an R8 recovery path; B25 gains a local instance of the independent-support rule.

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): receipts and artifact links on the seeded history 2 engineer-days (depends on P4 landing); fault injection and verifiers 2 engineer-days; rollback planner and support check 3 engineer-days. Model: 3 arms x 2 diagnosis modes x 60 cases x about 6 calls x about 3k tokens, about 6.5M tokens in 4 shards; estimated 10 to 15 USD per shard. Cost M.
