claim: Narrowed from the card to one attributable change. On build-time questions over chunked long engineering artifacts, with the candidate pool fixed per question, admitting passages by an absolute threshold on Jev's 4-level usefulness score (probability-weighted score at least 1.5 of 3, as GPT Researcher ships it, not refit) raises the per-question precision of the passages handed to the agent by at least 10 points over ranking by the same score and filling the same token budget, where a question with nothing handed over scores precision 0, while losing no more than 3 points of needed-evidence recall against the retriever's own order. Source: N-20260929-02 (GPT Researcher context filter, repo-pinned): on 28 web research tasks, Jev's thresholded filter kept 73% relevant passages against 46% for text-embedding-3-small cosine at 0.42, 15-10-3 in pairwise report preference, and "the threshold is the source of the gain" (Jev without threshold, top 10: 50%). The note's limits shape this spec: precision is a ratio over kept chunks, so a filter that keeps fewer is favoured (hence the per-question estimand with empty scored 0), and relevant text was slightly lower for Jev (3.3k vs 3.5k tokens); recall of needed evidence was never measured; the baseline was a weak bi-encoder; no cross-encoder or listwise arm; the writer and judge share a family; `jev-latest` unpinned; web research pages, not engineering artifacts. This is the pruning our synthesis advises against (B11), so the test is whether the precision gain survives a recall check on our reads. The result applies to the tested threshold of 1.5 only.

arms (same questions, same frozen candidate pool P (our hybrid retriever, RRF k = 60, top 40 chunks of 1,000 characters), same 4,000-token hand-over budget, same packing rule: passages in the arm's order until the budget is full, a passage that would overflow is skipped):
- A (control): retriever order, no rerank.
- C (strongest cheap reranker): local cross-encoder bge-reranker-v2-m3 (pinned revision), ordered by score. Reported with intervals and used only for the rerank_harm_bounded outcome; it takes no part in the admission decision.
- E-rank: Jev 4-level usefulness score per passage (question text and level descriptions copied from GPT Researcher at its pinned commit), ordered by probability-weighted score, no threshold.
- E-admit (decision arm): the same Jev scores, only passages scoring at least 1.5 admitted, ordered by score; may hand over fewer tokens than the budget, or nothing (admission must be allowed to be empty, B11). E-admit minus E-rank is the attributable change: the threshold alone, on identical calls.
- B-cos (reported, no decision): cosine similarity from the retriever's own embeddings at the source's 0.42 cut, to connect to the source's baseline. The 0.42 cut is the source's, not calibrated to our embedder, so B-cos may be handicapped; no conclusion is drawn from it.
- override: the card's arm D (one Jev listwise choice over the pool) is E-20260926-06-r2's question and is not re-run here. The card's sufficiency noul that expands to the next 30 candidates when below 0.5 is dropped: it changes the candidate pool and the stopping rule in the same round, which is H8's question (B9).

metric: needed-evidence recall within the hand-over budget (primary for harm): per question, labelled needed spans covered by handed passages / labelled needed spans (a span counts as covered when at least 80% of its characters are inside handed passages). Precision (primary for the gain), predeclared estimand: per-question precision = handed passages overlapping any needed span / handed passages, with precision set to 0 for a question where the arm hands over nothing (every question in the set has at least one needed span, so an empty hand-over is always a miss); the arm's precision is the unweighted mean over all 180 questions, and no question is ever dropped from the mean or from the bootstrap. Reported beside it: the pooled ratio estimator (sum of overlapping passages / sum of handed passages), the conditional mean over non-empty questions, and the count of empty hand-overs per arm. Also reported: a blind human check of 200 handed passages that overlap no labelled span, to estimate how often "imprecise" passages are in fact useful; handed tokens; Jev tokens, USD and latency per question.

predeclared margin: admit_positive needs precision(E-admit) minus precision(E-rank) at least 10 points with the two-sided 95% interval excluding zero, and the one-sided 95% lower bound of recall(E-admit) minus recall(A) above minus 3 points. rerank_harm_bounded needs the one-sided 95% upper bound of recall(C) minus recall(A) below +2 points. The card's comparison against every other arm is narrowed to E-rank (same scores) and A (the recall reference); B-cos is reported with intervals.

sample size and power note: 180 questions: the 120 frozen PDLC questions of PDLC-SEED-ASF plus 60 build-time questions written for this spec, all written before any arm runs; needed spans annotated by two engineers, adjudicated. Clustering unit: the source artifact of the question's first needed span (about 60 artifacts). Recall: assuming a per-question SD of the paired recall difference of about 0.15 and a design effect of about 1.5, SE is about 1.4 points; at a true difference of 0, the one-sided lower bound clears minus 3 points with probability Phi(3 / 1.4 minus 1.645), about 0.69. Precision (empty scored 0): per-question SD of the paired difference about 0.28 under this estimand (the zeros widen it), design effect 1.5, SE about 2.6 points; the rule needs the mean at or above 10 points and the interval excluding zero, so the binding condition is the 10-point mean; at a true 10-point gain P(pass) is about 0.5, at a true 15-point gain about 0.97. These are normal approximations; with about 60 clusters of unequal size, the harness first runs `docq power` (a pre-registered simulation that resamples artifacts from the frozen label set under the assumed effects, 2,000 draws, seed 20261109) and records the simulated pass probabilities in `power.json` before any Jev call; the decision rule does not change with the simulation. Jev run-to-run variation is measured by re-scoring P for 40 questions; the threshold decision is reported for passages within 0.1 of 1.5.

kill criterion: admit_negative if recall(E-admit) minus recall(A) is below minus 5 points and its 95% interval lies wholly below zero. Invalid (rerun under a new spec revision, not a verdict): span agreement between annotators (span-level F1) below 0.6; questions or spans edited after any arm ran; the pool manifest differing between arms; the Jev question text or version differing from the pin; any question missing from an arm's per-question file.

what a pass unlocks: a measured answer, on our build-time reads, to whether the source threshold of 1.5 (not a refit threshold) is what buys precision, for the R9 placement at the reranking stage (priority 1) and for B11 (working); either Jev admission at 1.5 as a separate step in shadow, or ranking-only kept as the operational default; 180 questions with needed-evidence spans for the PDLC memory benchmark (priority 4). It does not select Jev over a thresholded local scorer; that is untested here.

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): 60 new questions and needed spans for 180 questions, 2 engineers x 4 days; 200-passage human check, 0.5 day; harness 2 engineer-days (reuses E-20260928-18-r2's PDLC-SEED-ASF loader and E-20260928-19-r2's packer). Model tokens: Jev 180 x 40 x about 400 = 2.9M, plus repeats 40 x 40 x 400 = 0.64M; cross-encoder 180 x 40 x about 350 = 2.5M local; total about 6.0M tokens in 5 shards (below), aggregate hard cap 7.0M. USD: Jev about 0.15 USD; GPU rent about 1 hour, under 5 USD; aggregate cap 15 USD. Cost S in money, M in people.
