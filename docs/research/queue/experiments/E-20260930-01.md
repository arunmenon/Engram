claim: With the store, ingestion, supersession decisions, retrieval budget and reader held fixed, serving superseded values with explicit validity dates and the supersedes link (supersede-and-retain) raises previous-value accuracy by at least 15 points over serving only the current value (supersede-and-hide) while costing no more than 3 points of current-value accuracy; and the two arms' overall accuracy differs by less than their previous-value gap, so a single accuracy number would hide the difference. Source: N-20260929-20 (MemProbe, arXiv 2609.30558, EMNLP 2026, MIT code and data pinned at 9068d8d): under one shared reader and typed-field scoring with no LLM judge, Graphiti and LangMem tie overall (62.5 vs 65.2, paired bootstrap p = 0.534; all three mid-range systems pairwise p > 0.37) but differ on previous-value 96.4 vs 67.9 (p = 0.002, surviving Bonferroni over 18), while Graphiti trails on current-value 66.1 vs 80.4 (p = 0.041, not Bonferroni-significant). Limits that make our test necessary: that was a whole-system comparison of two products, not a serving-policy toggle; Graphiti's adapter returned top-15 edge facts without validity timestamps (so old and new facts sat side by side undated, which plausibly explains its weaker current-value); only 28 previous-value probes per system (the 28.6-point gap is 8 probes); one run per system; about 16% of Graphiti session-adds were lost to malformed extraction JSON; synthetic chat episodes with atomic facts, not long PDLC artifacts. Our arms differ only in the read-time serving rule, which is the isolated question the source could not answer.

arms (one store instance per episode, built once and shared by both arms: same ingestion through our write path, same supersession decisions from our current linker recorded in the P5 `superseded` state, same hybrid retrieval with the same served-token budget of 2,000 tokens, same reader and reader prompt):
- A (supersede-and-hide, control): superseded items are excluded from the served context; only current items are served.
- B (supersede-and-retain): superseded items that retrieval returns are served as well, each rendered with `valid_from` (its `recorded_at`), `valid_to` (the superseding item's `recorded_at`) and `superseded_by: <id>`; current items are rendered with `valid_from` and `valid_to: current`. Items keep their retrieval order; the rendering adds at most 30 tokens per item and is counted inside the budget.
- R (reported, no decision): MemProbe's full-context reader baseline on suite56, to place both arms against the source's reference row (88.8 in the paper).
- override: the card's "3 runs per arm" is kept for the reader only; ingestion runs once per episode and is shared, because repeating ingestion would change the store between arms and make the serving rule unattributable. Reader repeats estimate reader variance.

metric: per arm, accuracy on current-value probes, previous-value probes and change-detection probes, each scored by MemProbe's `score_answer()` typed-field comparison against gold (no LLM judge); reported beside them: overall accuracy, MemProbe's Plasticity, Stability and SP-Balance, source and conflict-value probes, served tokens, and the share of probes where the superseding link is missing (our linker missed the update), which bounds how much any serving rule can do.

predeclared margin: positive needs (B minus A) previous-value accuracy at least 15 points with the two-sided 95% interval excluding zero, and (B minus A) current-value accuracy above minus 3 points as a point estimate with its one-sided 95% lower bound reported. The overall-accuracy masking check is descriptive: report whether |overall(B) minus overall(A)| is smaller than the previous-value gap.

sample size and power note: previous-value probes: 28 in suite56 plus 30 in the PDLC episodes (one per episode), 58 in total; current-value probes: those in suite56 (the repo count is recorded in `power.json` at freeze) plus 30 PDLC. Clustering unit: episode (56 plus 30 = 86). Previous-value: if hiding makes the reader answer "unknown" or the new value on most previous-value probes, discordance between arms is high (0.3 to 0.5); SE of the paired difference about sqrt(0.4 / 58) x sqrt(1.2), about 9 points; a true 30-point gain (the size of the source gap) passes the 15-point rule with probability about 0.95, a true 20-point gain about 0.7, a true 15-point gain about 0.5. Current-value: discordance is expected small (only probes whose retrieved set contains a superseded item can differ), about 0.08 on roughly 60 to 90 probes, SE about 3 to 3.5 points; the current-value side is a screen for a large loss, not a non-inferiority test at 3 points, and a true 0 difference fires the 3-point point-estimate kill with probability about 0.2. This is the run's exploration slot and is labelled a pilot for the current-value side; its paired variances size a confirmatory test.

kill criterion: negative if (B minus A) current-value accuracy is below minus 3 points (point estimate, the card's rule), or (B minus A) previous-value accuracy is below 5 points (point estimate). Invalid (rerun under a new spec revision, not a verdict): the served store differing between arms for any episode (checked by per-episode store sha256); served tokens over budget in any probe; the reader, reader prompt or MemProbe scoring code differing from the pin; gold labels of the PDLC episodes edited after any arm ran; fewer than 25 PDLC episodes passing the authoring toolkit's validation.

what a pass unlocks: a measured read-surface policy for supersession (R2), where engineering agents need both "what is the requirement now" and "what did we decide before"; a scoring rule for the PDLC memory benchmark (priority 4) that reports current-value, previous-value and change-detection separately; a local measurement beside B3 (lead), which is about write-time supersede edges: this spec holds the edges fixed and tests how they are served, so it narrows what B3's knowledge-update gain can depend on; the missing-link share tells E-20260926-07-r2 how often our linker fails to record an update.

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): 30 PDLC change episodes authored by T1 with MemProbe's toolkit (a requirement, design decision or constraint changes mid-history; current, previous and change-detection probes with gold), 3 engineer-days, second engineer validates gold, 1 day; adapter for our store (MemProbe's `reset`, `ingest`, `retrieve`) 2 engineer-days. Model tokens: ingestion through our extraction model, 1,246 suite56 sessions plus about 450 PDLC sessions, about 1,700 sessions x about 1.6k = 2.7M; reader: about 314 probes x 2 arms x 3 repeats x about 2.4k = 4.5M, plus R on suite56 once, about 0.2M after truncation to its published setup; total about 7.4M in 7 shards (the ingestion and reader totals are recorded at freeze; if the reader total exceeds plan, repeats drop from 3 to 2 before any other change and the drop is written to `run_manifest.json`).
