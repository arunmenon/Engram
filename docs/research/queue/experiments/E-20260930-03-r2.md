claim: On engineering-memory questions over a PDLC project history, at a small served budget (about 300 tokens), storing raw ledger events and asking Jev one yes or no relevance question per shortlisted event is non-inferior within 3 points of answer accuracy to write-time LLM extraction, both as we run it today (facts in retriever order) and with the extracted facts ranked by the same Jev question (the source's main comparator), and the Jev selector drops all shortlisted gold evidence for at most 10% of the questions whose shortlist contains gold evidence; at a large budget (about 1,500 tokens) the comparison is reported, with extraction expected to lead. Source: N-SURVEY-20260929-01 (arXiv 2609.34227, pre-registered, code pinned by tag; paper-single-benchmark): on LoCoMo held-out conversations at about 250 served tokens, raw turns plus one Jev noul per shortlisted turn scored 77.0% against 77.5% for gpt-4o-mini fact extraction plus Jev (difference minus 0.5, one-sided bound minus 3.0 against a registered minus 5 margin; human strict minus 1.7, bound minus 3.9), at 0.0006 against 1.865 USD write cost per 1,000 turns and 273 ms against 816 ms read p50 versus an LLM reranker; at 20 served items extraction won (82.4 against 77.6). On evidence loss the source's denominators are, per the challenger's check of the shortlist-recall analysis (REV-20260930-09): "Among questions with evidence in the shortlist, the rerank kept none of it for 10.4%", and across all questions the rerank's contribution was 9.2%. Plain cosine kept more correct abstention at k = 3 (63.6 against 54.1). Limits that make our test necessary: chat memory, not engineering artifacts; judge, answerer, LLM reranker and extractor all gpt-4o-mini; human audit by an author, on discordant items only; closed jev-1.13.0 only. The source's name clash (its repository is called Engram) is unrelated to us. N-SURVEY-20260929-05 (checklist for System One claims) shapes the arms: repeat-call consistency reported, latency with caching off, and no combining of separate noul probabilities as a distribution.

arms (same segmented ledger, same questions, same answerer and answer prompt, same judge; recall or no-recall gate held fixed and separate: the answerer is always called and its prompt allows "not in memory"; every arm packs units in its order until the budget is full, applying the frozen overflow rule (a unit that would overflow is skipped and packing continues with the next unit that fits); two predeclared budgets, S = 300 and L = 1,500 served tokens):
- A (raw floor): raw ledger segments, shortlist of 30 by our hybrid retriever (BM25 plus vector, RRF k = 60), packed in retriever order, no selector.
- B (decision arm): A's shortlist, one Jev noul per segment with the source's wording (question "Does memory help answer query?"; true option "It states or directly implies part of the answer."; false option "It is off-topic or only shares a keyword."), packed by P(true).
- C (reported): the same noul on laya-typed-decisions served locally through Unsloth's Jev-compatible API, ranking only, segments over its window counted and ranked last.
- D (baseline 1, current production): our current write-time LLM extraction over the same ledger (production extractor and prompt, pinned), facts retrieved by the same hybrid retriever (shortlist 30), packed in retriever order.
- D-J (baseline 2, strongest extraction configuration): D's shortlist ranked by the same Jev noul; the source's main matched-budget comparator. B minus D-J isolates the representation with the selector fixed.
- segmentation (applies to A, B, C): raw events are split at stage F0, before any model call, into segments of at most 140 tokens (pinned tokenizer) at sentence boundaries, each segment keeping its event id; no raw unit can exceed half of budget S. Facts are not segmented (the stage F1 freeze records their length distribution; any fact over 140 tokens is reported, and is packed under the same overflow rule).
- override: the card's H1 DreamBench-SWE sequences are dropped: executable-oracle agent runs on 60 three-session sequences for five arms at two budgets would need tens of millions of tokens, beyond POLICY-v1.1's 12 runs a week; this spec measures answer accuracy on questions, the source's own endpoint. The card's label log-probability arm on an open backbone is not run: E-20260929-03-r2 already compares open decision models with Jev on our decisions.

metric: answer accuracy (primary), per budget, judged against the gold short answer by a judge from a different model family than the answerer, with abstention on the 30 unanswerable questions scored correct only when the answer says the information is not in memory; selector evidence drop (decisive for B) = among answerable questions whose shortlist contains at least one gold-bearing unit, the share whose packed set contains none; overall evidence-dropped = among all answerable questions, the share whose packed set contains no gold-bearing unit (reported per arm); a raw segment is gold-bearing if it overlaps an answer-bearing span; a fact is gold-bearing only if the fact-level label says it states the answer-bearing content of a span (parent event id alone does not count); budget fill = served tokens / budget per question; correct abstention rate; write cost per 1,000 events; read p50 and p95 latency with caching off; Jev repeat flip rate on 40 questions; a blind human check of the judge on 150 answers, stratified by arm.

predeclared margin: positive at budget S needs the one-sided 95% lower bounds of accuracy(B) minus accuracy(D) and of accuracy(B) minus accuracy(D-J) both above minus 3 points (intersection-union, no multiplicity correction needed), and B's selector evidence drop at most 10% (point estimate). Budget L and every other contrast are reported with intervals and do not decide.

sample size and power note: 200 questions: the 120 frozen PDLC questions of PDLC-SEED-ASF plus 80 written for this spec, of which 30 are unanswerable; all written, with gold answers, gold evidence events and answer-bearing spans, before any model call. Clustering unit: the source artifact of the question's first gold evidence unit (about 60 to 70). At a discordance of about 0.15 on 170 answerable questions and a design effect of about 1.3, SE of each paired accuracy difference is about 3.4 points; at a true difference of 0 from both baselines, each non-inferiority bound is met with probability about 0.22 and both with less (they are positively correlated); at a true 8-point loss against either, the negative rule fires with probability about 0.65. ## Revision notes

Review REV-20260930-09 (outcome revise) on E-20260930-03; each finding and what changed:
- [numbers] The spec said the rerank "dropped all evidence for 10.4% of questions"; the source's 10.4% is among questions with evidence in the shortlist, and its all-question contribution is 9.2%. Changed: the claim quotes both figures with their denominators; the per-category 22.7% and 31.4% figures are removed because their denominators were not rechecked; the decisive metric is now defined on the source's denominator (selector evidence drop, among questions whose shortlist holds gold), with the all-question rate reported.
- [baseline] D-J, the source's main comparator, was report-only, so a pass against D could end extraction while D-J was better. Changed: D-J is now a decisive baseline; positive needs non-inferiority against both D and D-J (intersection-union), and negative fires on a clear loss against either.
- [alternative] At 300 tokens, whole raw units over the budget were skipped while facts are shorter, so loss could be an accident of unit size. Changed: raw events are segmented at F0 by a frozen rule into units of at most 140 tokens; the overflow rule (skip and continue) is frozen and identical across arms; length distributions per representation and budget fill per question are recorded and reported.
- [leakage] A fact's parent event id does not prove it kept the answer-bearing content. Changed: answer-bearing spans are written at F0; a fact counts as gold-bearing only through a fact-level label (written at F1 by labellers who see facts and spans, never arm outputs); a raw segment counts only if it overlaps a span.
- [numbers] The confirmatory size of about 600 was wrong; the spec's own inputs give about 1,340. Changed: corrected to about 1,340 answerable questions per comparison, and the verdict quotes only the stage F0 simulation with the intersection-union rule.
- [packet] Stage 0 required all hashes before calls although facts are produced by extraction; missing code, data, pins and manifests; freeze order invalid. Changed: the work is ordered F0 (no model calls) then P1 to P3 (extraction, embeddings, shortlists) then F1 (no model calls: facts, fact labels, shortlists frozen) then E1 to E7; each freeze has its own bundle and challenger check before the next model call. override: executable harness code cannot exist before the CTO accepts the spec and T3 builds it; the spec makes the populated freeze bundles a reviewed precondition of the first model call instead.
- [injection] GPT Researcher and Lore material contains setup instructions. No change needed: none of that material is used by this spec, and nothing from it is executed.
