claim: A System One model can decide whether a pull request implements a requirement or design section close enough to an LLM checker to be used at the PDLC linking stage. Specifically, on 200 held-out human-labelled (requirement or design section, PR) pairs from SpecMine Kiro repositories, at least one of hosted Jev, a self-hosted open-weight decision model, or a fine-tuned ModernBERT-large reaches macro-F1 on the 3-way label (implements / partial / no) within 10 points of a single-call LLM checker, or a Jev-then-LLM confidence cascade comes within 2 points of the LLM checker at under 50% of its cost. Sources: N-SURVEY-20260928-14 (explicit PR-issue links misaligned in 13.6% of SWE-bench Verified; frontier multi-agent checker 88.11% to 92.12% binary accuracy at USD 0.08 to 0.23 per link; human kappa 0.575 on the fine-grained label; no System One arm in any source), N-SURVEY-20260928-11 (Jev 11.6 macro-F1 points behind the best LLM at 44x lower cost on social-science tasks; cascade at 27% to 56% of LLM cost), N-SURVEY-20260928-13 (SpecMine data), N-SURVEY-20260928-12 (fine-tuned encoder close to a bespoke read-out). The card lists no belief; the register has none on trace-link deciders. The spec tags B14 because the auto-write threshold rests on calibration measured here.

arms (same pairs, same rubric; neutral option names A/B/C, following N-SURVEY-20260928-10, so the result does not depend on E-20260928-06's outcome):
- A: hosted Jev, one Choice decision over three neutral-named options with rubrics for implements / partial / no.
- B: a self-hosted open-weight decision model, Laya-0.4B (Apache 2.0); decider-0.8b replaces it only if its licence is confirmed before freeze. Same Choice.
- C: ModernBERT-large fine-tuned as a 3-way classifier on SPECMINE-LINK-TRAIN (below), from repositories disjoint from the evaluation repositories.
- D: LLM checker: one call to a pinned Claude model (claude-sonnet-5, version pinned at freeze) with the PAIChecker misalignment taxonomy in the prompt and the same three labels. This is the quality reference, not PAIChecker itself (which is multi-agent and costs more).
- E: cascade: A, then D for every pair whose A confidence is below a threshold fit on the calibration split to maximise macro-F1 subject to D-call share <= 50%.
- Controls: a rule arm (explicit reference or co-change only, no model) and the label base rate, both reported.
- Three-option instability (N-SURVEY-20260928-10 reports it on Laya even with neutral names) is checked by also reporting A and B as two binary decisions (any link? then full or partial?) on the test split; the verdict uses the Choice form and states the gap.

metric: macro-F1 on the 3-way label (primary); precision at recall 0.8 for the binary "any link" collapse (implements or partial vs no); ECE after one temperature refit per arm on the calibration split; cost per 1,000 links at list prices on the run date (C and B: amortised GPU time); p95 decision latency; whether pair text left our infrastructure (A, D, E: yes; B, C: no).

predeclared margin: 10 macro-F1 points for a single System One arm against D; 2 points and < 50% of D's cost for E. These are the card's margins; they are practical margins, not significance thresholds.

sample size and power note: 300 human-labelled pairs from 6 to 10 repositories, split by repository into 100 calibration (temperature, cascade threshold) and 200 test. Half are candidate links (co-change or typed reference), half hard negatives (same repository, same period, not linked). Two labellers label every pair; kappa is reported; disagreements are adjudicated by a third. At n = 200 the 95% interval on a macro-F1 difference is roughly +/- 6 to 8 points (inference, cluster bootstrap by repository with 6 to 10 clusters will be wider), so the 10-point margin is resolvable only when the observed gap is well away from it; results near the margin are reported inconclusive, not as a pass. The human ceiling (kappa 0.575 on a 4-class variant) caps achievable macro-F1; report every arm against labeller agreement. The 1,500 training pairs for C carry silver labels (agreement of co-change and typed references, the SpecMine recipe), with a 100-pair human audit of silver-label accuracy reported; card said "training split" without saying how it is labelled; override: 1,500 human labels are out of budget for an S-to-M test.

kill criterion: A, B and C all more than 10 macro-F1 points below D on the test split, and E not within 2 points of D at under 50% of D's cost: System One is killed for the IMPLEMENTS decision this quarter. Also invalid (rerun, not a verdict) if labeller kappa on the 3-way label is below 0.4.

what a pass unlocks: The CTO's priority 3 slice: IMPLEMENTS edges written on every pull request by a model we can afford and, for B or C, host ourselves so the text stays in-house (priority 2); an auto-write confidence threshold fit on our data rather than vendor calibration (B14); 300 labelled pairs that seed family F3 of the PDLC memory benchmark (PC-SCOPE-20260928-01, deferred) and H7's typed records.

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): about 300 pairs x 5 arms plus the binary variants, about 2,500 decisions; D is the largest spend (a single call per pair; PAIChecker's multi-agent cost of USD 0.08 to 0.23 per link is an upper bound, so under USD 100 for D, estimate); ModernBERT fine-tune on one GPU for a few hours; labelling 300 pairs x 2 labellers at about 5 minutes each, about 50 labeller hours plus adjudication; 5 engineer-days for extraction, harness and fine-tune. Cost M.
