claim: For Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (pinned, self-hosted) used as a memory reranker at pool size 20, scoring each candidate separately from next-token logits (pointwise, P) gives higher recall@1 than one listwise call scored from next-token logits over single-token candidate labels (LL), by at least 5 points pooled and with a positive point estimate on each dataset. Separately, at pool size 200, neither Jev's choice call nor Qwen pointwise scoring gives a material recall@5 gain over no rerank (paired upper bound below 3 points). Both claims are pilot-scoped to LoCoMo-200 and PDLC-SEED-ASF.

Sources and what they do and do not show. N-SURVEY-20261003-07 (recommendation reranking study): at K=20 Jev's single choice call had the best MRR (0.238 / 0.295 / 0.346 on Movies, Video Games, Books), Qwen2.5-7B pointwise was close (0.232 / 0.259 / 0.282) and Qwen listwise was worst (0.199 / 0.213 / 0.245); at K=200 on Movies no LLM arm beat SASRec without rerank (MRR 0.093 vs Jev 0.080). In the source, both Qwen designs read next-token logits over single-token candidate labels (section 4, Appendix B: "Neither pointwise nor listwise reranking requires free-form text generation") and candidate order is randomised deterministically. Limits: recommendation, not memory; one held-out item as ground truth; users filtered to targets inside SASRec's top 200; no cross-encoder; call counts and cost not stated; latency not hardware-normalised; text and tables disagree on large-K claims (table values used). E-20260926-06-r2 has no open instruct-model listwise vs pointwise arm and no pool sweep; not merged. B10 is already scoped to one vendor and LoCoMo-200, so this pilot can only annotate it for one open model on two sets.

arms (same frozen candidate pools from the pinned hybrid retriever, same memory text truncated to 120 tokens per item, same question text; one frozen random candidate order per (question, K), seed 20261116, shared by every LLM arm):
- R (baseline): no rerank, retriever order.
- J: hosted Jev (pinned version), one choice call with the K items as alternatives in the shared order; scores = returned probabilities.
- LL (source-faithful listwise): Qwen one call listing the K items with single-token labels in the shared order; score of each item = its label's next-token logit, renormalised over the K labels; no text generation, no parser. K=20 only (single-token labels are not available for K=200 under the pinned tokenizer).
- LG (generated listwise, reported only): Qwen one call returning a ranked list of labels; frozen parser; unparsed or missing items are appended in a fixed random order (seed 20261116), never retriever order; unparsed rate recorded. K=20 only. It isolates the effect of generation and parsing from call shape and is not part of either decision rule.
- P (pointwise): Qwen one call per item, score = P("yes") from next-token distribution restricted to yes/no and renormalised.
- pool sizes: K=20 on all 160 questions with R, J, LL, LG, P; K=200 on one shared 40-question LoCoMo subset with R, J, P (the same 40 for every arm, membership frozen in `subsets.json`). K=50 is dropped.

metric: primary, recall@1 at K=20; recall@5 at K=200; also MRR, calls per question, tokens, p95 latency (Jev hosted including network; Qwen on the pinned GPU host, reported separately and not compared head-to-head), GPU-hours.
- multi-gold: recall@1 counts a hit when any gold memory id is ranked first; recall@5 counts a hit when any gold id is in the top 5; MRR uses the best-ranked gold id. Questions with no gold id in the pool are excluded at freeze and counted.

predeclared margin:
- claim 1 (call shape, K=20): let d = recall@1(P) minus recall@1(LL), paired, 95% cluster bootstrap. positive_pointwise: d.mean at least 0.05, d.lo above 0, and the point estimate of d above 0 on each dataset separately. negative: not positive, and recall@1(LL) minus recall@1(P) has lower bound above 0. inconclusive otherwise, including a pooled pass with one dataset's point estimate at or below 0 (recorded as dataset_disagreement).
- claim 2 (large pool, K=200), per arm a in {J, P} that was measured: e_a = recall@5(a) minus recall@5(R), paired, one-sided 95% cluster bootstrap. refuted if any measured arm has e_a.lo above 0; else no_material_gain if every arm has e_a.hi below 0.03 and both arms were measured; else unclear. An arm not run (budget or failure) is reported "unknown" and claim 2 cannot be no_material_gain. The three outcomes are mutually exclusive by evaluation order.

sample size and power note: 100 LoCoMo-200 questions (seed 20261116, about 10 conversations) and 60 PDLC-SEED-ASF questions (about 15 Decisions or components) at K=20; 40 LoCoMo questions at K=200. Clustering unit: conversation or component. Corrected from r1: at discordance 0.25, design effect 1.5 and n=160, the SE of the K=20 paired difference is 0.0484, so a two-sided 95% lower bound above zero clears with probability about 0.18 at a true 5-point gap and about 0.54 at a true 10-point gap, before extra uncertainty from about 25 clusters. This is a pilot that can detect only a large call-shape effect and sizes a confirmatory run; at K=200 with 40 questions the 3-point bound is unlikely to clear unless both arms are close to R, so "unclear" is the expected outcome there unless a gain is large. `tl power` runs before any model call and is recorded.

kill criterion: negative for claim 1 as above. Invalid: pools, candidate orders or subset membership differing from the frozen files; truncation or prompts differing from the pin; Qwen weights hash, serving config or Jev version differing; any LL label not a single token under the pinned tokenizer; per-arm failure rate above 3% (LG's unparsed items count as reported, not failures); fewer than 150 questions scored in R, J, LL and P at K=20.

what a pass unlocks: the call shape for a self-hosted reranker on our two datasets (priority 2: run our own System One model so memory text stays in-house); a dataset-scoped annotation on B10 (lead); a first reading of whether reranking pays at K=200, which informs the pool cap for whatever model owns the stage.

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): harness 2 engineer-days (pools, orders, four rerank adapters, scorer). No labelling (LoCoMo gold evidence; PDLC seed questions carry gold Decision ids from H7's fixture). Tokens: K=20, listwise about 2.6k per question: J 0.42M, LL 0.42M, LG 0.42M; P 160 x 20 x 0.2k = 0.64M; K=200 on 40 questions: J 40 x 25k = 1.0M, P 40 x 200 x 0.2k = 1.6M; total about 4.5M, cap 5M, measured with the pinned tokenizer at freeze. USD: Jev about 8. GPU: Qwen arms measured on the pinned host, estimated 3 to 4 GPU-hours, capped at 6. Five runs. Cost S.
