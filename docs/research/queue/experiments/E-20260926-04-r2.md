claim: At a fixed served-token ceiling and identical candidate pool, task-conditioned curation at Spec time (H3) does not exceed anchored hybrid retrieval (top BM25 items first, then RRF fill) by 3 or more points of spec-task success; and a curator used as a reranker adds value only if it beats a non-curator cross-encoder reranker on the same candidates.

arms:
- Arm A (strong-retrieval control): anchored hybrid: top-k BM25 hits first (k fixed on dev, default 5), then RRF (k=60) over vector, BM25 and entity channels, in that fused order.
- Arm B: H3 task-conditioned curation selecting from the same candidate pool.
- Arm C: Arm A's candidate pool reordered by the curator's scores.
- Arm D (added control): Arm A's candidate pool reordered by a non-curator cross-encoder reranker (bge-reranker-v2-m3, local, pinned). C is judged against D, so a gain from generic reranking is not credited to the curator.
- Shared across arms: candidate pool = top 50 from Arm A's retrieval (retrieval depth pinned); packing policy = whole items in each arm's rank order, skip an item that does not fit and continue, stop when the remaining budget is below the smallest remaining item; the served-token ceiling S is fixed on dev from {2,000, 4,000, 8,000} (default 4,000) and counted with the executor's tokenizer; actual served tokens are logged per task. Arms are matched on the served-token ceiling, not on total inference spend; curator and reranker cost is reported separately and any cost claim uses total spend.
- Same frozen executor and judge as H3. Executor, curator and judge are pinned to different models; the judge never sees arm labels or retrieval metadata.

metric: spec-task success as scored by the frozen H3 judge under H3's success rule (binary). Secondary: evidence recall on tasks with gold evidence; actual served tokens; total inference tokens and cost per task including curator or reranker; curator and reranker latency.

predeclared margin: two primary comparisons, Holm-adjusted at family-wise 0.05: (1) B minus A; (2) C minus D. ship_B requires diff_BA >= 0.03 and the adjusted lower bound > 0. keep_C requires the adjusted lower bound of diff_CD > 0 and diff_CA >= 0.01. bound_B (evidence that curation's gain is below 3 points) requires the 95% upper bound of diff_BA < 0.03. Failing ship_B is not evidence of equivalence.

sample size and power note: the source's +1.79 points was not significant (p = 0.478). For paired binary outcomes with 15 to 20% discordance, detecting 3 points at 80% power needs roughly 1,300 to 1,750 tasks; the H3 set is expected to be a few hundred, and at n = 200 only about 8 to 9 points are detectable. The spec therefore sets an operational default (Arm A, cheaper and deterministic) when neither ship_B nor bound_B holds, and the verdict must say "not detectable at this n". Inference always clusters by repository; if fewer than 8 test repositories exist, the verdict is reported as underpowered. McNemar is dropped as a decision statistic because it assumes independent pairs.

kill criterion: diff_BA < 0 on the sealed test split (point estimate): curation is not shipped as a selector and survives only if keep_C holds. Stop before running (not a result) if the BM25 channel fails the preflight (P1: dead RediSearch prefix) or any pin is missing.
