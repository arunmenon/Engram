claim: Narrowed from the card to one attributable change. On build-time questions over chunked long engineering artifacts, with the candidate pool fixed per question, admitting passages by an absolute threshold on Jev's 4-level usefulness score (probability-weighted score at least 1.5 of 3, as GPT Researcher ships it) raises the precision of the passages handed to the agent by at least 10 points over ranking by the same score and filling the same token budget, while losing no more than 3 points of needed-evidence recall against the retriever's own order. Source: N-20260929-02 (GPT Researcher context filter, repo-pinned): on 28 web research tasks, Jev's thresholded filter kept 73% relevant passages against 46% for text-embedding-3-small cosine at 0.42, 15-10-3 in pairwise report preference, and "the threshold is the source of the gain" (Jev without threshold, top 10: 50%). The note's limits shape this spec: precision is a ratio over kept chunks, so a filter that keeps fewer is favoured, and relevant text was slightly lower for Jev (3.3k vs 3.5k tokens); recall of needed evidence was never measured; the baseline was a weak bi-encoder; no cross-encoder or listwise arm; the writer and judge share a family; `jev-latest` unpinned; web research pages, not engineering artifacts. This is the pruning our synthesis advises against (B11), so the test is whether the precision gain survives a recall check on our reads.

arms (same questions, same frozen candidate pool P (our hybrid retriever, RRF k = 60, top 40 chunks of 1,000 characters), same 4,000-token hand-over budget, same packing rule: passages in the arm's order until the budget is full, a passage that would overflow is skipped):
- A (control): retriever order, no rerank.
- C (strongest cheap reranker): local cross-encoder bge-reranker-v2-m3 (pinned revision), ordered by score.
- E-rank: Jev 4-level usefulness score per passage (question text and level descriptions copied from GPT Researcher at its pinned commit), ordered by probability-weighted score, no threshold.
- E-admit (decision arm): the same Jev scores, only passages scoring at least 1.5 admitted, ordered by score; may hand over fewer tokens than the budget, or nothing (admission must be allowed to be empty, B11). E-admit minus E-rank is the attributable change: the threshold alone, on identical calls.
- B-cos (reported, no decision): cosine similarity from the retriever's own embeddings at the source's 0.42 cut, to connect to the source's baseline.
- override: the card's arm D (one Jev listwise choice over the pool) is E-20260926-06-r2's question and is not re-run here. The card's sufficiency noul that expands to the next 30 candidates when below 0.5 (from jev-for-finance) is dropped: it changes the candidate pool and the stopping rule in the same round, which is H8's question (B9), and bundling it would make any gain unattributable.

metric: needed-evidence recall within the hand-over budget (primary for harm): per question, labelled needed spans covered by handed passages / labelled needed spans (a span counts as covered when at least 80% of its characters are inside handed passages); precision of handed passages: handed passages overlapping any needed span / handed passages (questions where an arm hands nothing count as precision undefined and recall 0, and their count is reported); a blind human check of 200 handed passages that overlap no labelled span, to estimate how often "imprecise" passages are in fact useful; handed tokens; Jev tokens, USD and latency per question.

predeclared margin: admit_positive needs precision(E-admit) minus precision(E-rank) at least 10 points with the two-sided 95% interval excluding zero, and the one-sided 95% lower bound of recall(E-admit) minus recall(A) above minus 3 points. The card's comparison against every other arm is narrowed to E-rank (same scores) and A (the recall reference); C and B-cos are reported with intervals.

sample size and power note: 180 questions: the 120 frozen PDLC questions of PDLC-SEED-ASF plus 60 build-time questions written for this spec ("what constraint applies before changing X", "which decision governs Y", "what did the design say about Z"), all written before any arm runs; needed spans annotated by two engineers, adjudicated. Clustering unit: the source artifact of the question's first needed span (about 60 artifacts). Recall: assuming a per-question SD of the paired recall difference of about 0.15 and a design effect of about 1.5, SE is about 0.15 / sqrt(180) x sqrt(1.5), 1.4 points; at a true difference of 0, the lower bound clears minus 3 points with probability Phi(3 / 1.4 minus 1.645), about 0.69. Precision: per-question SD about 0.25, SE about 2.3 points; a true 10-point gain passes the interval rule with probability above 0.99 and the 10-point point rule with probability about 0.5, a true 15-point gain about 0.98. Jev run-to-run variation is measured by re-scoring P for 40 questions; the threshold decision is reported for passages within 0.1 of 1.5.

kill criterion: admit_negative if recall(E-admit) minus recall(A) is below minus 5 points and its 95% interval lies wholly below zero. rerank_off is reported alongside whatever the admission result is. Invalid (rerun under a new spec revision, not a verdict): span agreement between annotators (span-level F1) below 0.6; questions or spans edited after any arm ran; the pool manifest differing between arms; the Jev question text or version differing from the pin.

what a pass unlocks: a measured answer, on our build-time reads, to the question the note says is the source of GPT Researcher's gain (threshold admission, not ranking), for the R9 placement at the reranking stage (priority 1) and for B11 (working); either admission-by-threshold as a separate step in shadow, or ranking-only confirmed with the retriever order as the baseline; 180 questions with needed-evidence spans for the PDLC memory benchmark (priority 4).

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): 60 new questions and needed spans for 180 questions, 2 engineers x 4 days; 200-passage human check, 0.5 day; harness 2 engineer-days (reuses E-20260928-18-r2's PDLC-SEED-ASF loader and E-20260928-19-r2's packer). Model tokens: Jev 180 questions x 40 passages x about 400 tokens = 2.9M, plus 40 repeats x 40 x 400 = 0.64M; cross-encoder 180 x 40 x about 350 = 2.5M local.
