claim: On memory gate decisions from our own pipeline (write-gate Noul `quote_supports_item` and Choice `source_type`, recall-gate Noul), a frozen-encoder student fitted to Jev's probability distributions, with a kNN out-of-distribution gate and a threshold chosen by a 95%-confidence Clopper-Pearson bound at target 98% agreement, answers at least 50% of held-out calls locally while the share of all calls answered locally and different from Jev stays at or under 2% on at least 19 of 20 seeded splits; the same cascade with a point-estimate threshold exceeds 2% on more splits. Source: N-20261008-01 (Jevstiller, repo-pinned at f5f52e7, single author, not executed by us): at target 98%, held-out coverage 69.0 to 80.2% on three many-class intent and topic tasks but 22.2 to 24.1% on two noisy tasks, with the bound rule exceeding the budget on 1 of 100 splits against 6 to 12 of 20 per task for the point estimate, at 4 to 8 points less coverage. The note's correction applies: 1/100 exceedance is within the stated 95% confidence, and agreement with Jev is not accuracy. Transfer is the open question: the source tasks are short-text classification, ours are multi-field memory states with two or three questions per call, so coverage may sit nearer the noisy-task 22% than the 70% headline.

Dataset deviation from the card, stated: the card asks for a log of our own recorded Jev gate decisions. No dogfood tenant is named under POLICY-v1.1, so recorded production decisions cannot be used. The log is built instead by running pinned Jev on gate inputs our own extractor and recall path produce over permitted data (seeded T1 PDLC history and LongMemEval-S sessions). It is our pipeline's decision distribution on permitted text, not production traffic; the verdict says so.

arms:
- A (baseline): every call goes to Jev (pinned version); the student is not consulted. Coverage 0%, disagreement 0% by definition; downstream reference.
- B: Jevstiller-style cascade, one student per question: frozen bge-small encoder (pinned revision) on the rendered gate state, logistic head trained on Jev's soft labels, kNN OOD gate (reference set = fitting rows, k = 10, distance cut = 99th percentile of fitting-row neighbour distance), threshold chosen strictest-first on a fixed grid by the Clopper-Pearson upper bound (95%) on the joint rate "answered locally and different from Jev" over all calibration calls, target 98%; a 2% random audit slice always goes to Jev and is excluded from coverage.
- C: identical to B except the threshold is the loosest grid point whose point-estimate joint disagreement on calibration is at or under 2%.
- Score `strength` is not distilled; it always goes to Jev in every arm and is reported only as the share of calls the student cannot save.

metric: per question and pooled: held-out local coverage; joint disagreement over all held-out calls (mean, worst split, number of splits above 2%). Secondary, gated on P3: write-gate F1 against H5 labels for A versus B on the 90 H5 items, and recall@5 on the P3 memory eval set with the recall gate served by A versus B. Statistic: per split values over 20 seeded splits (fit 2,000 / calibrate 1,000 / hold out 2,000 per split, split by source project and time, seeds 0 to 19); report median and range, and a Clopper-Pearson 95% interval on the count of violating splits.

predeclared margin: B passes if pooled median held-out coverage >= 50% and at most 1 of 20 splits has joint disagreement above 2%. The bound-versus-point contrast counts if C has at least 3 more violating splits than B.

sample size and power note: 2,000 held-out calls per split give a binomial SE on a 2% rate of about 0.31 points, so a split at 2.6% is reliably distinguished from 2.0%. 20 splits are correlated (shared pool) and are a stability check, not 20 independent replications; the source used the same design. Coverage per question is reported separately; a question with fewer than 1,500 rows is reported descriptively only.

kill criterion: pooled median coverage of B below 30%, or B violates the 2% budget on more than 2 of 20 splits, or (when the gated secondary runs) recall@5 with B more than 1 point below A or H5 write-gate F1 with B more than 2 points below A.

what a pass unlocks: a measured agreement contract for keeping most gate text in-house with no new model hosting (CPU only); local evidence for B14 (bound-fitted thresholds versus point estimates) and for B13 (escalation evaluated on the routed population); a shadow deployment spec.

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): 4 runs: 3 collection runs of about 1,700 gate calls each (state capped at 800 input tokens plus up to 100 output tokens, about 1.6M tokens and an estimated under 10 USD per run at pinned Jev prices) and 1 CPU-only fitting and replay run (no model calls). About 4.8M tokens total, under 30 USD, within the weekly cap. People-days: 2 to port the cascade (fit, OOD gate, bound threshold, audit) onto our rendered gate state, 1 for the collection harness, 1 for analysis.
