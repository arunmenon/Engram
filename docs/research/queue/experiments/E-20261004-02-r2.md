claim: On build-time questions over our seeded project history, when the served memory contains one superseded Decision rewritten to contradict the current one plus at least one later item that reflects the current state (the misleading condition), an admission gate run by Jev Noul before the executor, using only deployable inputs, cuts the executor's wrong-answer rate by at least 20 points against the executor's own abstain instruction, while falsely abstaining on at most 15% of the questions the executor answers correctly with gold memory (cluster-aware one-sided 95% upper bound below 20%).

Sources and what they do and do not show. N-SURVEY-20261003-04: with one entity-swapped passage among five, an explicit abstain prompt (P1) still answered 13.6% to 74.3% of the time across small quantised models. Correction from r1: 59.6% is the echo rate pooled over methods P0 to P4 (Table 3), not the P1-only rate; the abstract says 63%. Correction from r1: P3 is the conflict check (answer rate 13.3%, false abstention 49.8%) and P4 is the NLI check, which uses a closed-book answer as its reference (14.0%, 31.0%). AURC did not improve (0.434, 0.451, 0.452); the swap audit found 13% of swaps broken. Limits: small quantised models only, no trained gate, no typed decision model, QC-eligible questions are easier, code promised but not released. No register experiment or open spec tests admission under a misleading memory: H5 measures extraction precision, E-20260926-05-r2 tests a none option and a recall gate on unanswerable prompts, E-20261003-01-r2 tests poison authority; this is not merged.

conditions (same 120 questions, each built in four context variants of five memories; every item carries text, created_at and a provenance quote, the fields the store already returns):
- G gold: gold Decision plus four related negatives; answerable.
- I all-irrelevant.
- R degraded: gold plus hard negatives and three related distractors.
- M misleading: the current Decision replaced by a superseded Decision rewritten to contradict it, plus at least one later item (ticket, pull request or design note, dated after the superseded Decision) whose text reflects the current state without stating the answer; correct behaviour is to abstain. This makes the misleading item detectable from deployable inputs. An M0 subvariant on 30 questions omits the later item and is reported as a floor (no deployable signal), not decided.
- authoring: one person blind to arm outputs, from real Decision supersessions in the seeded history where they exist (target at least 60 of 120); each M item audited for a well-formed contradiction and for the later item not leaking the answer string; broken items replaced before freeze.

gate inputs (deployable, answer-key free; `key.jsonl` is read only by the grader):
- every gate sees: the question, the five items with created_at and provenance quote; nothing derived from the key.

arms (same pinned executor model and abstain instruction in every arm; the gate removes items before the executor sees them; if all items are removed the executor receives an empty context and must abstain):
- P (baseline): executor's explicit abstain instruction, no gate.
- L (source-faithful NLI control, after the source's P4): the executor model answers the question closed-book once (frozen prompt); DeBERTa-v3-large MNLI (pinned hash) scores each item as premise against that answer as hypothesis; an item is removed if contradiction probability exceeds t_L. The closed-book answer is fallible by design, as in the source.
- C (source-faithful conflict control, after the source's P3): the same NLI model scores every ordered item pair; an item is removed if its maximum contradiction probability against any later-dated item exceeds t_C.
- N (decision arm): Jev Noul `item_supported_and_current` (pinned version, frozen text, neutral option names): state = question, the candidate item with created_at and provenance quote, and the other four items with created_at; question = "Is this item supported by its quote and not contradicted by a later item?"; item admitted if P(yes) at least t_N.
- O (open-weight arm): override: dropped from this revision to pay for gated fit runs; open-versus-hosted decision quality is covered by E-20260929-03-r2 and can be added here in a later revision if N passes.
- threshold grid: each of t_L, t_C, t_N over {0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 0.9}; objective on the fit split: minimise wrong-answer rate on M plus false abstention on G, equal weights, both measured by actually running the executor on each distinct gated fit context (identical gated contexts across thresholds are run once and reused); ties go to the higher threshold.

metric: primary, wrong-answer rate on M = share of M questions where the executor gives a substantive wrong answer (includes echoing the superseded value), graded against the frozen key by a frozen string-and-entity grader with a human audit of 60 graded items. False abstention on G = share of the capable set that the arm abstains on, where the capable set is G test questions that P answers correctly and that have valid output in every arm. Also answer rate and echo rate per condition, AURC, gate p95 latency, calls and tokens per question.
- AURC confidence score: for gate arms, the maximum gate score among admitted items (N: P(yes); L: 1 minus contradiction probability; C: 1 minus maximum pairwise contradiction), 0 when nothing is admitted; questions are ordered by that score and risk is the wrong-answer indicator over all four conditions. P has no score; it is reported as a single coverage-risk point, not an AURC.

predeclared margin: for each gate X: pass[X] needs wrong(P, M) minus wrong(X, M) at least 0.20 with the one-sided 95% cluster-bootstrap lower bound above 0.10, and false abstention(X, G) at most 0.15 with one-sided 95% cluster-bootstrap upper bound below 0.20, with the capable set holding at least 30 questions in at least 15 Decision clusters. If the capable set is smaller, false abstention is "unmeasurable" and X cannot pass. Outcome: positive if pass[N]; negative if not positive and (wrong(P, M) minus wrong(N, M) below 0.10, or the false-abstention lower bound above 0.20); inconclusive otherwise. Preference among passing gates: N is preferred over a passing L or C only if its false abstention on G is lower by at least 0.05 (greater than or equal to 0.05); otherwise the cheaper passing gate (L before C) is preferred and the outcome records it. The frontmatter outcomes follow the preferred gate.

sample size and power note: 60 fit and 60 test questions per condition (split by seeded project component, seed 20261109), clustered by underlying Decision (about 40 test clusters). wrong(P, M) is expected near 0.5 and a working gate near 0.15; paired discordance about 0.4 gives SE about 0.065, so the 0.10 lower bound clears with probability about 0.9 at a 0.35 true gain and about 0.5 at a 0.20 gain. The false-abstention denominator is the capable set, expected 40 to 50 of 60 if P answers 70% to 85% of G correctly; with cluster-aware bounds and about 30 clusters, a true rate of 0.08 clears the 0.20 bound with probability about 0.65, so joint pass power at the expected effect is about 0.55. This is a pilot; `tl power` (cluster resampling on the frozen capable-set structure after the fit split is run) is recorded and does not change the rule.

kill criterion: negative for N as above. Invalid: context items edited after any arm ran; any gate input reading `key.jsonl`; contradiction audit pass rate below 0.9 on M; later-item leakage audit failure on any M item not replaced; grader human-audit agreement below 0.9; thresholds fitted on test questions; pins differ; per-arm failure rate above 3%; fewer than 54 scored test questions per condition in all arms.

what a pass unlocks: which component owns admission in /v1/context (Noul gate vs prompted abstention vs NLI or conflict controls), before H5's scorer earns production shadow; B11 (working: admission is separate and must allow empty) gains a local measurement on a stale Decision; a stale-decision family for the PDLC memory benchmark (priority 4). Promotion to shadow is a separate reviewed spec.

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): fixture build 3.5 engineer-days (variants, later items, both audits, key); gate arms 2 engineer-days; grader and audit 1 day. Model tokens: Jev scoring fit and test, 480 contexts x 5 items x about 0.6k = 1.44M; closed-book answers 120 x 0.5k = 0.06M; gated fit executor runs, at most 200 distinct gated contexts per gate x 3 gates x 3k = 1.8M plus P fit 120 x 3k = 0.36M; test executor 4 arms x 240 contexts x 3k = 2.9M; NLI local; total about 6.6M in 6 shards, cap 7M. USD about 65 at a frontier executor, under 15 per shard. Cost M.
