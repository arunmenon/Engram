claim: On our labelled escalation set, the input-feature escalation classifier's leave-one-repository-out (LORO) AUC is at least 0.10 below its item-level AUC and within 0.03 of a per-repository base-rate oracle, while a deferral rule on tier-1 output features keeps at least 0.05 more AUC than the input-feature classifier under LORO.

arms:
- Arm A: input-feature escalation classifier (the H5 cascade's escalation step as currently designed), trained and scored with item-level stratified 5-fold cross-validation.
- Arm A-LORO: the same classifier and features, trained on all repositories but one and scored on the held-out one, for each repository.
- Arm A-shuffle: Arm A's item-level protocol with labels permuted within each repository (base rate per repository preserved), 20 permutations.
- Oracle: per-repository base-rate predictor (predicts the training escalation rate of the item's repository; under LORO, the pooled training rate).
- Arm B: deferral rule on the tier-1 output (answer length in tokens, abstain or hedge markers, confidence spread across tier-1 components), logistic regression, scored LORO.

metric: AUC for the label "tier 2 changes the decision" (label as in H5's cascade: 1 if the tier-2 decision differs from tier 1 and matches the gold label). Secondary: shuffle-retention ratio = AUC(A-shuffle) / AUC(A); label test-retest kappa on a 50-item relabel to set the achievable ceiling.

predeclared margin: group memorisation declared if AUC(A) minus AUC(A-LORO) >= 0.10 AND AUC(A-LORO) minus AUC(Oracle) <= 0.03, OR shuffle retention >= 0.85. Output features preferred if AUC(B) minus AUC(A-LORO) >= 0.05 with the repository-clustered interval excluding zero.

sample size and power note: minimum 6 repositories and 300 labelled turns, with at least 60 positives overall and at least 5 positives per repository (repositories below that are pooled into an "other" group and reported). With 6 clusters the LORO interval is wide: a repository-clustered bootstrap on 300 items gives an AUC standard error of roughly 0.04 to 0.05, so a 0.10 gap is detectable but a 0.03 equivalence to the oracle is only suggestive. The equivalence arm is therefore reported with its interval and does not by itself change a gate. Labels must be written by someone other than the classifier author (as in H5).

kill criterion: AUC(A-LORO) within 0.03 of AUC(A): no group effect in our traffic; stop and record the negative outcome. Also stop before running if the labelled set has fewer than 6 repositories or fewer than 60 positives (record as not runnable, not as a result).

what a pass unlocks: a mandatory group-held-out protocol (LORO + shuffle + oracle) for every decision gate before its verdict, including H5 and H10b; escalation redesigned to key on tier-1 output, which costs no extra inference. Moves B13 (lead) with our own measurement.

cost (runs, tokens, people-days): S. Offline: classifiers train on cached features; tier-1 outputs already exist from H5 shadow. Inference only for any missing tier-1 outputs (at most 300 calls). About 1.5 people-days for the harness and the 50-item relabel. Budget: policy unset.
