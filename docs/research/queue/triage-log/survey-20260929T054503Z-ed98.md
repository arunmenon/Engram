## Contradictions (first)

1. **Isotonic recalibration of a System One gate can make it worse, and thresholds fitted on a calibration split did not keep their error budget.** An independent pre-registered reproduction of Laya (arXiv 2609.33843) found isotonic, the calibration-split winner, had the worst held-out NLL (0.7738 vs 0.7027 uncalibrated); a single temperature worked (ECE15 0.2035 to 0.0372). The registered 10% accepted-set error target failed on test (9.2% on the calibration split, 14.0% on test). For Jev on security decisions (arXiv 2609.33401), temperature scaling worsened ECE (0.104 to 0.134) or NLL (0.481 to 2.163) on two of three benchmarks. This contradicts the design assumption in PC-20260926-04 (isotonic recalibration lowers ECE by at least 2x) and qualifies B14's "fit thresholds locally": fitting locally is necessary but not sufficient. Note N-SURVEY-20260929-02, which also **revises N-SURVEY-20260928-11**: Laya's miscalibration is under-confidence (confidence 0.553 vs accuracy 0.767) and the shipped temperatures soften in the wrong direction.
2. **Links imported from tools are not gold, and links do not stay valid.** About 10% of human-maintained requirement links in an industrial project were invalid (219 of 2,132, arXiv 2504.15427); 70% of 6.7 million distinct commit-message links no longer resolve (arXiv 2305.16591). The pdlc-memory-layer draft has no re-validation step for a link once written. Not a register belief; a design assumption. Note N-SURVEY-20260929-07, card 03.
3. **A large model is not needed to rank trace-link candidates.** A LightGBM ranker with repository metadata beat GPT-4o and GPT-OSS-120B agents by about 25 F1 points on issue-to-commit links (73.17 vs 48.39, arXiv 2607.23610). Contradicts the implicit assumption in PC-SURVEY-20260928-06 that the candidates are decided by a model. Note N-SURVEY-20260929-06, card 02.
4. **The typed readout has shown no independent accuracy advantage** over the same backbone reading label tokens (audit of 28 papers, arXiv 2609.32160), and Jev's separate yes/no probabilities for three labels sum to 1.14 on average (arXiv 2609.33209). Any gate that adds or compares separate Noul probabilities is affected. Note N-SURVEY-20260929-05.

No register belief (B1 to B25) is contradicted outright. B7 and B11 are supported by N-SURVEY-20260929-01; B14 and B20 by N-SURVEY-20260929-05.
