B1 still holds if the receipt also keeps the model output, model id, provider and sampling parameters, and "reproduce" means "show inputs and the recorded output". Suggested restatement: B1 holds for artifact replay only; decision re-derivation needs recorded outputs or batch-invariant self-hosted inference. Card PC-SURVEY-20260927-01.
2. **B17 (R5, working): a feedback-loop measurement now exists outside the survey.** RoMeRL (N-SURVEY-20260927-05, arXiv 2608.02508) planted 10 % noisy memories. Under MemRL's outcome-linked joint-credit rule, each noisy entry got 3.7 positive utility updates over ten rounds; with UCB exploration it got 7.2. The experience-following study (N-SURVEY-20260927-04, arXiv 2505.16067, ACL 2026) found that ungated add-all memory scored below no learning on all four agents (e.g. RegAgent 67.53 to 55.48). B17 stays literally true for the September survey's systems but should not be read as "unmeasured anywhere". Card PC-SURVEY-20260927-02.

The gain to 66.8 came from validation and consolidation. Single run, not coding, not cross-lineage. It does not overturn MGM, but it means H6 as designed cannot separate comparative evidence from a validation effect. Card PC-SURVEY-20260927-04.
