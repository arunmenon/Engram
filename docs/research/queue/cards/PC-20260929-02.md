claim: On our build-time queries over chunked long artifacts, a thresholded Jev usefulness score raises the precision of the passages handed to the agent by at least 10 points over embedding cosine filtering, a cross-encoder and no reranking, without lowering recall of the needed evidence by more than 3 points.
proposed experiment: Candidates fixed per query by our hybrid retriever (RRF k=60, top 40). Arms: (A) retriever order, no rerank, top-k at the token budget; (B) embedding cosine filter, top-k; (C) local cross-encoder rerank, top-k; (D) one Jev listwise choice over the pool, top-k (no pruning); (E) Jev 4-level usefulness score per passage with a fixed threshold (1.5 of 3, as in GPT Researcher) plus a Jev sufficiency noul that expands to the next 30 when below 0.5 (as in jev-for-finance). Dataset: frozen set of at least 150 engineering-agent build-time questions with the needed evidence spans labelled by a person. Metrics: recall of needed evidence within the token budget (primary), precision of handed-over passages, tokens, latency, $ per query; relevance labels by people, not an LLM judge. Margin: E beats A, B, C and D on precision by at least 10 points with recall no more than 3 points below the best arm. Kill: E's recall falls more than 5 points below A, or no arm beats A (the no-rerank control) on recall, in which case reranking stays off.
why it matters for us: This tests an R9 placement at the reranking stage and the R3/R4 split between ranking and admission (B11): GPT Researcher's gain came from a pruning threshold our synthesis advises against, and RSI-Jev v3.0 shows a decision-model reranker scoring below no reranking at all. A result either licenses admission-by-threshold on our read path or confirms ranking-only with the retriever order as the baseline to beat.
