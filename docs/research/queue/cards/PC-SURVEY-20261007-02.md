claim: Real backends expose lifecycle state inconsistently (Mem0 overwrites; FR-Bank marks 5,968 entries stale vs about 102 in the reference; A-MEM's 23,186 links resolve to no note), so a content-only swap test misses staleness changes (arXiv 2610.04794).
proposed experiment: Add to P0 step 5 a lifecycle fixture: seed superseded, retracted and expired items, swap adapters, and compare arm A (adapter returns content and provenance only) vs arm B (adapter also returns lifecycle_state and a store count manifest) on stale-answer rate for "current" questions. Pass: arm B shows zero stale-as-current answers across both backends.
why it matters for us: P0 currently verifies content, provenance, abstention, scope and failure semantics, but not lifecycle; this is the gap the census shows real stores fall into.
