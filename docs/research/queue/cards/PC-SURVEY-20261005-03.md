claim: jev-state reports 74.3% to 100% accuracy from projecting raw state into labels before a Jev call, but its projection uses the exact thresholds and words of the label rules (pinned at 3c30e89), so the lift is largely by construction.

proposed experiment: Refines H5. On the 90 labelled extracted items, three arms with the same instructions: raw item plus context; projection with fields pruned and lists capped but no derived labels; projection with labels from thresholds fitted on the fitting half only. Measure precision and recall, reliability by band and input tokens on the test half.

why it matters for us: A projection layer is cheap to build, but we only know decision thresholds after fitting. This separates the gain from trimming noise (portable) from the gain from pre-computing the answer (not portable).
