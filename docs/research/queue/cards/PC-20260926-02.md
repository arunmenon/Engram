claim: On our labelled escalation set, the input-feature escalation classifier's leave-one-repository-out AUC is at least 0.10 below its item-level AUC and within 0.03 of a per-repository base-rate oracle, while an output-feature deferral rule keeps at least 0.05 more AUC under leave-one-repository-out.
proposed experiment: Arm A = input-feature escalation classifier (current H5 design) evaluated item-level 5-fold; Arm A' = same classifier evaluated leave-one-repository-out and with labels shuffled within repository; Arm B = deferral rule on the tier-1 output (answer length, abstain markers, confidence spread) evaluated leave-one-repository-out. Dataset: the H5 labelled turns grouped by repository (at least 6 repositories, 300 turns). Metric: AUC for "tier 2 changes the decision", plus shuffle-retention ratio. Predeclared margin: if Arm A' shuffle retains >= 85% of Arm A AUC, the classifier is declared group-memorising. Kill: if leave-one-repository-out AUC of Arm A is within 0.03 of item-level, drop this card (no group effect).
why it matters for us: R6 requires that an evaluation support its conclusion; a gate that only recognises which repository a turn came from will fail on the next repository. The result decides how every R4 gate in the stack is evaluated before it ships.
