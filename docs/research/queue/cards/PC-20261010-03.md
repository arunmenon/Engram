claim: On our own labelled write-gate and supersession decisions, a ModernBERT-size encoder fine-tuned with the typed-decisions recipe matches hosted Jev on the routing-style questions, while a local LLM logprob readout is needed for exact-match questions (does this memory cite this id and value).
proposed experiment: Arms: A hosted Jev zero-shot; B Laya zero-shot; C ModernBERT-base fine-tuned (shuffled question order, cross-entropy plus Brier) on our training groups; D Qwen3-4B logprob readout with 3 option rotations. All arms temperature-scaled out-of-fold with folds grouped by source session. Dataset: at least 600 labelled decisions (save/skip, kind, supersedes-which, exact-match check), held-out by session. Metrics: accuracy, AUROC, ECE after scaling, errors above 0.9, CPU latency. Margin: C or D within 2 pp accuracy and 0.03 AUROC of A per question type. Kill: no local arm within margin on save/skip.
why it matters for us: Priority 2 is running a decision model ourselves so memory text stays in-house; R9 needs the stage-by-stage answer on our data, and the source shows zero-shot open ports fail while fine-tuning splits by question type. R4 calibration must be fitted locally whatever wins.
