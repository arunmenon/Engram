claim: Consolidation that keeps every raw observation and calls the summariser only when merge or promote is selected with probability >= 0.85 and contradiction < 0.85 is no worse than consolidation off on hit@5 and knowledge-update accuracy, while ungated cluster merging (Jaccard >= 0.35, minimum cluster 2, merged summary replaces its members at read time) loses at least 2 points of hit@5.

proposed experiment: Arm A = consolidation off. Arm B = ungated cluster merge in the Hippo style (episodic memories clustered by Jaccard >= 0.35, minimum cluster 2, merged summary served in place of members). Arm C = Jev-Mem-style gated consolidation: every 20 writes, a keep_separate / merge / promote / uncertain choice over a bounded neighbourhood; the summariser runs only when merge or promote is selected with probability >= 0.85 and contradiction < 0.85; raw observations stay retrievable and the summary is added, never substituted. Consolidation unit in B and C is a group of related entries, not a single write (Supermemory's "coherent units, never one isolated write"). Schedule held fixed across arms so only the mechanism varies (H9's trigger comparison stays separate). Dataset: H2's harness, LongMemEval stores plus the knowledge-update subset. Metric: hit@5 under ONE scorer declared before the run, plus knowledge-update accuracy, served tokens and number of Summary nodes read. Also report the two other scorers as secondary, because Hippo's -3.6 pp [-5.8, -1.4] hit@5 was under a text-credit scorer and was +0.4 and +2.2 under others. Predeclared margin: C non-inferior to A within the noise floor on both metrics; B below A by at least 2 points hit@5 on the declared scorer. Kill: C below A beyond the noise floor (then summarisation is cache only, as H9 already says), or B within the noise floor of A (the Hippo result does not replicate on our stack, and the gate is not needed).

why it matters for us: R2 needs to know whether Consumer 4's summaries earn their keep, and the only measured on/off result in the catalogue (Hippo, "merge is the first suspect") is for merging that replaces its inputs, while Jev-Mem's non-destructive, confidence-gated design has no on/off number at all. Separating these two keeps B5's negative finding from being applied to a mechanism it never tested.
