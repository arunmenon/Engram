claim: On our corpus, a single listwise decision-model choice call over the fused pool beats a local cross-encoder by at least 3 points of end answer accuracy at 5 served memories, not only on recall@1.

proposed experiment: Arm A = one choice call over the whole pool, rank positions used as scores (Hindsight design). Arm B = one yes/no call per candidate, batched into one request, sorted by probability (the pointwise design Hindsight measured against). Arm C = local cross-encoder ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2 (Hindsight's default and Hippo's fallback). Arm D = one self-hosted reranker from the SciFact chart (bge-v2-m3 or mxbai-large). Candidate order and pool identical across arms; PC-20260926-03's order control applies to Arm A. Dataset: P3 held-out set plus 100 build-time read queries from P0 fixtures, 30 and 240 candidates per query. Metrics: end answer accuracy by the frozen judge at 2 and at 5 served memories (primary), recall@1, recall@5, cost per query, p50 and p95 latency. Predeclared margin: A >= C + 3 points answer accuracy at 5 memories with a paired, task-clustered interval excluding 0. Kill: A within the noise floor of C or D at 5 memories (then the hosted call's only benefit is a shorter context and its egress risk is not justified), or A below B on recall@1 (B10 does not replicate).

The notes disagree on what the rerank buys. Hindsight: recall@1 0.94 listwise vs 0.87 pointwise at a thirtieth of the calls on 200 LoCoMo questions, and 0.950 vs 0.800 against local MiniLM at 30 candidates (0.583 to 0.783 at 240). Hippo: R@1 0.6167 vs cross-encoder 0.4133 on a private 300-query store, but graded answers at 5 memories were tied (Jev vs CE -0.0067; "An answer win over the free cross-encoder was not shown"), with the gain appearing only at 2 memories (base 0.0867, CE 0.1067, Jev 0.1400). Supermemory: Jev Score-10 at approx. 0.746 nDCG@10 on SciFact against approx. 0.735 to 0.766 for self-hosted rerankers at roughly a tenth of the cost.

why it matters for us: R4 asks that a decision model take over only where it beats the cheap alternative on the outcome we care about, and every positive rerank number in the catalogue is a retrieval metric while the one answer-level test was a tie. The result decides whether the rerank sends memory text to a hosted API at all.
