claim: A trained sufficiency stop kept 55.17 of 56.49 (AMA-Bench) while stopping early on 30% of queries, but stayed below always-deep on BEAM (42.09 vs 43.26) and never reported wrong stops; a production RAG study found an untrained "retrieval returned nothing" trigger escalated only 27.8% of queries and beat always-escalate.
proposed experiment: Refines H8. Add two arms to H8's harness with identical hard caps: (a) trivial post-retrieval trigger (continue only when top hit score is below a floor or hits are empty); (b) Jev-Mem sufficiency stop with Engram-fit thresholds; existing fixed-k arm. Report accuracy, retrieved tokens, rounds, and false-sufficiency rate (early stops whose answer is wrong but correct under the deep path) per arm, with the threshold fit on half and tested on half.
why it matters for us: If the trivial trigger matches the learned stop, B4 needs no decision-model call on the read path, saving latency and keeping memory text in-house.
