claim: With one entity-swapped passage among five, an explicit "abstain if insufficient" prompt still answered 13.6% to 74.3% of the time and 59.6% of answers echoed the planted entity; NLI and closed-book checks cut this to about 14% but falsely abstained on 31.0% to 49.8% of answerable questions, with no gain in risk-coverage area.
proposed experiment: Refines H5 and H10(b). Build three admission conditions from a seeded project history: all-irrelevant memories, degraded (gold plus hard negatives), and misleading (one superseded Decision rewritten to contradict the current one, among negatives). Arms: prompted abstain in the executor; DeBERTa NLI contradiction gate; Jev Noul `quote_supports_item` gate. Metrics per condition: wrong-answer rate when it should abstain, false abstention on items answered correctly with gold, AURC.
why it matters for us: Build-time agents act on admitted memory; empty-context tests overstate safety, and the misleading case is exactly a stale decision.
