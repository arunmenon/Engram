claim: Ten locally correct cases led reflection-memory agents to store over-general rules (77.43% stored, 59.14% caused failures, GPT-4o, Math); an LLM auditor left 40.29% attack success, and harm persisted after 50 benign queries (arXiv 2605.18930). No reinforcement rate was measured.

proposed experiment: Refines H4 by adding a seeded stressor. Into H4's replayed task stream, plant OEP-style case bundles (correct per case, wrong as a rule) drawn from our own task history. Arms: access-count reinforcement; outcome-linked reinforcement; outcome-linked plus a cross-context-support requirement before a rule is promoted. Metrics: rule-stored rate, harmful-retrieval rate and H4's feedback-loop rate (fraction of wrong memories whose stability rises), with and without P5 provenance tags.

why it matters for us: H4 assumes outcome links make reinforcement safer. This stressor tests whether outcome links reinforce wrong rules that happen to succeed locally, which is the failure B18 warns about.
