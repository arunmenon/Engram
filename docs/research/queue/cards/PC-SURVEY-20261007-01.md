claim: With evidence held fixed, the reader-facing format moves memory QA accuracy by 24.6 to 48.8 points across deployed-style templates, and formal ledger packets make three of nine readers score 0 % (arXiv 2608.23568).
proposed experiment: On the P3 harness, hold retrieval and budget fixed and run two arms that differ only in renderer: our current typed briefing vs a natural-language rendering of the same items, on LongMemEval-S plus our PDLC items, two readers (one per family). Metric: accuracy and refusal rate; kill if the gap is within the re-judge noise floor (about 3 answers in 500, N-SURVEY-20261007-01).
why it matters for us: H2 and H3 compare retrieval and curation choices; if renderer effects are this large, an unfrozen renderer would swamp those comparisons.
