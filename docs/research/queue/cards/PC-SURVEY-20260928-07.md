claim: The kinetic half of an ontology (typed actions whose preconditions are checked against the graph before an agent acts) is what our ontology lacks. The mechanism is credible from outside the PDLC. Deterministic predicates cost 1.11 to 2.83 ms (AgentSpec). A 4B guard matched GPT-5.4 on step-level safety (83.0 / 83.3 against 83.0 / 84.4 Acc / F1) at 599.9 ms per call (StepGuard). Palantir ships the shape as action types with submission criteria and a failure message per condition. Nobody has measured it on lifecycle preconditions.

proposed experiment:
- **Setup.** Seed a PDLC history (H7's seed of about 300 typed records: requirements, decisions, constraints, tickets, PRs, tests with VERIFIES, IMPLEMENTS, CONSTRAINS and SUPERSEDES). Define five actions: merge PR, close ticket, mark requirement verified, supersede decision, relax constraint. Write each one's preconditions as graph predicates (for example "no Requirement that this PR implements has a failing verifying Test", or "no active Constraint on a touched DesignElement"), plus one prose precondition per action ("respects the linked decision's rationale").
- **Cases.** Build 200 proposed actions, 80 of them violating at least one precondition, labelled blind by two engineers. At least 120 are benign, so the false-block bound is meaningful.
- **Arms.**
  - A: no check; the agent sees retrieved memory only and decides.
  - B: deterministic predicates on the Action type.
  - C: B plus a typed decision model on the prose precondition. Run it once with Jev and once with an open-weight model (Intern-Decision 4B or a 4B guard).
- **Metrics.** Violations caught, false blocks on benign actions, p95 added latency, and for A, the agent's own proceed or stop.
- **Kill criterion.**
  - B catches fewer than 70% of seeded violations, or its false-block one-sided 95% upper bound exceeds 5% on 120 benign actions: drop predicates as the primary mechanism.
  - C adds fewer than 10 points of violation recall over B, or adds more than 800 ms p95: ship rules only and record G14 as rules-only.
  - B is not at least 20 points of recall above A: the check adds nothing over good retrieval, so drop the Action type from ADR-0011.

why it matters for us: This decides whether the ontology amendment adds Action types at all, and whether a System One model has a job at the federation layer that is not in G1 to G13. It is also the most concrete product difference from a memory that only answers questions: the memory can say "stop, and here is why".
