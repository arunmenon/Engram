claim: Cross-fitted isotonic recalibration of the save/skip and injection-gate probabilities on our labels lowers per-source ECE by at least 2x and raises precision at fixed 80% coverage by at least 3 points compared with fixed thresholds on raw probabilities.
proposed experiment: Arm A = raw decision-model probabilities with fixed thresholds (content 0.5, injection 0.5); Arm B = isotonic recalibration fitted per source (chat turns, repo docs, tool output) on half the labels and scored on the other half, swapped and repeated 20 times; thresholds then chosen for 80% coverage. Dataset: at least 150 labelled save/skip turns and 150 labelled injection-gate lines per source (B21 sizes), labelled by someone other than the gate author. Metric: per-source ECE, calibration slope, precision at 80% coverage. Predeclared margin: ECE ratio A/B >= 2 on at least 2 of 3 sources and precision +3 points. Kill: ECE ratio < 1.3 on all sources, or precision change within +/- 1 point.
why it matters for us: R4 requires that we understand error costs before automating a decision; three independent sources this week (crash narratives 3.3x, RLCDAlignBench per-benchmark ECE 0.168, Banking77 ECE 0.097) say vendor probabilities are not calibrated per source. The result decides whether per-source recalibration is a required step in every gate.
