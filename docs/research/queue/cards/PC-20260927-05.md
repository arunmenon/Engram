claim: Replacing tool observations over 10 KiB with a stable ledger handle plus a 1 KB excerpt after their first two turns (exact bytes retrievable by handle) cuts input tokens per task by at least 20% without lowering task success by more than 2 points on our frozen coding-agent task set.
proposed experiment: Arm A = frozen harness, full observations kept in context (current behaviour); Arm B = same harness with ObservationPack-style handling (threshold 10 KiB, full for two provider requests, then handle plus 1 KB excerpt; a `fetch(handle)` tool returns exact chunks from the ledger). Dataset: the frozen P0 engineering task set, 3 seeds per arm, same model and version. Metrics: task success rate, total input tokens, number of `fetch(handle)` calls, replay check (every served excerpt resolves to ledger bytes). Predeclared margin: tokens -20% or better with success within -2 points (paired, 95% interval). Kill: success drops more than 2 points, or token saving under 10%.
why it matters for us: It is the cheapest test of whether the R1 receipt discipline (serve a pointer to an immutable record, not a copy) also pays for itself as R3 context budgeting. SoL-Pi's ObservationPack alone raised score on GPT-5.6 Sol (47.2 vs 44.8) while cutting tokens, so a null here would tell us the effect is harness- or backend-specific.
