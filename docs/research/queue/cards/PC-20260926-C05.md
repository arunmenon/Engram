claim: Inside one otherwise identical system, writing supersedes and contradicts edges at write time (and excluding superseded items from recall) raises knowledge-update accuracy by at least 5 points over the same system with every new unit simply added, at no loss on the other question classes beyond the noise floor.

proposed experiment: Arm A = edges off: "prefer add over modify" kept, every new unit added, no supersedes or contradicts edges, recall unchanged. Arm B = edges on: supersede only when two signals agree (contradiction >= 0.7 AND a named target id, jevmem's rule), old item kept but tagged superseded and excluded from recall, contradicts edges inhibited at read time (REALM's conflict_inhibit). Arm B2 = Arm B with the single-signal rule (contradiction alone), to test jevmem's "Contradiction needs two signals" design note. Everything else fixed: extractor, embedder, fusion, k, token budget. Dataset: H2's harness on the LongMemEval knowledge-update subset, plus a contradiction dev set built like jevmem's (reversals plus near-misses that must not supersede) from our own project history, labelled by someone other than the rule author. Metrics: knowledge-update accuracy, accuracy on the other LongMemEval classes, false-supersede rate on near-misses, missed-supersede rate on reversals. Predeclared margin: B >= A + 5 points on knowledge-update with a paired interval excluding 0, and false supersedes on near-misses no higher than 1 in 16 equivalent. Kill: B - A below the noise floor on knowledge-update (then REALM's 84.72 without reconsolidation vs Zep's 74.40 came from something other than the edges, and H7's typed-graph premise weakens), or B2 no worse than B on false supersedes (the two-signal rule is unnecessary).

Evidence behind the margin: REALM knowledge-update 84.72 without reconsolidation and 88.89 with it, against Zep's 74.40, but that is a whole-system comparison with baselines mostly copied from other papers; jevmem's contradiction dev set went from 20/27 to 25/27 reversals caught between v0.4.1 and v0.4.2 with 0/16 false supersedes, on an author-written set; Hippo's audit lists supersession among "what measured helpful" without an isolated number.

why it matters for us: R2 depends on write-time supersession being what makes current-state questions answerable, yet B3 rests on a comparison where the edges were never switched off. A clean on/off ablation tells H7 whether to seed its typed graph with SUPERSEDES edges as the core mechanism or treat them as an index.
