claim: In coding agents transferring memory across domains, distilled insights averaged 0.560 pass@3 versus 0.534 for raw trajectories and 0.523 for no memory, and raw trajectories hurt on two of six benchmarks (arXiv 2604.14004). This cuts against B16 for cross-project reuse.

proposed experiment: Refines H6 by adding the representation as the variable. Same Episode set, four arms (raw episode, workflow, summary, insight) crossed with two retrieval conditions (same task_key in the same project; different project). Frozen executor, executable oracles, predeclared margin, clustered intervals by task. Metric: re-use success and negative-transfer rate (arm below no-memory) per condition.

why it matters for us: B16 currently defers procedural memory. If raw wins only within a project and insights win across projects, Consumer 4 should keep both and route by project match rather than choose one.
