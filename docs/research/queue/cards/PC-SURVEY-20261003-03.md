claim: Jev's single choice call had the best MRR at 20 candidates in three domains (0.238 / 0.295 / 0.346), but an open Qwen2.5-7B asked listwise in one call was the worst LLM arm (0.199 / 0.213 / 0.245) and lost to the same model scored per item; at 200 candidates on Movies no reranking beat Jev (MRR 0.093 vs 0.080).
proposed experiment: Refines H5-rerank. On Engram's LoCoMo-200 and a PDLC seed set, add arms: open-weight model listwise single call; same model pointwise; Jev choice; no rerank. Sweep pool size 20, 50, 200. Metrics: recall@1, recall@5, calls, p95 latency.
why it matters for us: B10's "one call beats per-item" may be a Jev property; before self-hosting a reranker we need to know which call shape the open model needs and the pool size where reranking stops paying.
