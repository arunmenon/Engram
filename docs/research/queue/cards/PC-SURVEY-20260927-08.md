claim: SkillEvolBench ran 180 tasks across 6 environments with 10 models and 3 CLIs (Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI). Under frozen deployment, skills revised only after failures scored -2.56 pp vs no-skill (1 win, 1 tie, 8 losses), and curated static skills scored -2.44 pp. Raw-trajectory reuse scored 37.6% ESR vs a computed no-skill mean of 34.7%, above every skill arm's mean. Only update-after-every-attempt arms were slightly positive (+0.78 and +0.44 pp). Replay success overstated transfer by up to about 18 pp. Separately, HGM found that a lineage-pooled signal predicted descendants' performance better than an agent's own score (weighted r 0.778 vs 0.285 for DGM and 0.444 for SICA on SWE-Verified-60). HGM ran once, with no ablation separating the lineage signal from its other changes.

proposed experiment: This refines H6 and H3. H6 compares one-episode with multi-episode induction and scores re-use on the same `task_key`. This card adds the update-policy axis and a split of the held-out set.
Arms, on the H6 episode set:
- Arm A: raw top-3 episodes (no induction).
- Arm B: Workflow induced once, then frozen.
- Arm C: Workflow revised only after failures.
- Arm D: Workflow revised after every reuse.
Score on held-out variants split into three kinds:
- context shift
- adversarial shortcut (a tempting instance-specific path that is wrong)
- composition (two procedures chained)
Report replay-set success separately from held-out success, since replay inflates transfer.
Follow-on arm, only if C or D wins: choose which Workflow version to refine by the pooled reuse success of the version and its descendants (HGM-style), vs by its own success, on the same version tree.
Metrics: held-out success per split, instance-specific-value rate, tokens served.

why it matters for us: B16 now has a controlled benchmark behind it, including a code-debugging environment. If Arm A matches or beats B, C and D on held-out splits, Consumer 4 should keep raw episodes as the procedural read surface and treat induced Workflows as an index or cache. That would defer Workflow-as-truth. B15 stays lead: HGM supports lineage-pooled selection, not comparative refinement.
