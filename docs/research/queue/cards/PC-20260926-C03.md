claim: On our held-out set, a ranker with no "none" option that always returns at least one memory, placed behind a separate recall/no-recall gate, keeps gold retention within 0.02 of the ungated ranker on answerable queries and abstains correctly on unanswerable queries at least 20 points more often, whereas a ranker with a "none" option loses at least 0.05 gold retention.

proposed experiment: Arm A = choice rerank over the fused pool with no "none" option, at least 1 memory always served (Hindsight's shipped design). Arm B = the same choice with an added "none" option and a minimum probability of 0.05, serving nothing when nothing clears it (jevmem's shipped recall: "No listed memory is relevant to the query."). Arm C = Arm A behind a separate yes/no "does this prompt need memory?" gate evaluated before retrieval (Supermemory's UserPromptSubmit gate, which honours "without using memory, tell me..."). Dataset: P3 held-out set with answerable, unanswerable and explicit opt-out prompts, pools from H2's fusion. Metrics: gold retention and empty-result rate on answerable queries, correct abstention on unanswerable and opt-out prompts, end answer accuracy. Predeclared margins as in the claim. Kill: B's gold retention within 0.02 of A (the Hindsight failure does not reproduce on our pools, so the split in B11 is unnecessary), or C's abstention gain under 10 points (a pre-retrieval gate is not worth its call).

The notes disagree: Hindsight's "none of these" option emptied 35 of 200 questions and a "nothing is relevant" Score level emptied 7% of queries and dropped gold retention from 0.81 to 0.65, and Supermemory's Noul used as a keep/delete gate "kept nothing"; jevmem's design rule is the opposite ("Every choice has a `none` option") and it has no recall-quality measurement ("Recall quality is not measured"). Supermemory's recall gate has no accuracy, latency or cost numbers.

why it matters for us: R4 says a decision is automated only when its error behaviour is understood, and two shipped systems make opposite choices about where "nothing relevant" lives with no measurement on the abstention side. The result fixes the rerank-vs-admission split in B11 and tells P0 F10 whether an empty result should come from a gate or from the ranker.
