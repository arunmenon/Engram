## Net effect on the path forward

1. **P0 before everything**: the Spec-to-PR-review replay experiment with adversarial fixtures, two real backends, one harness. Pass is zero contract violations, not a benchmark score.
2. **Snapshot** is three named levels; the programme ratifies which one C6 means; unsupported levels are declared per backend, never faked.
3. **Ledger** = receipts, retained served artifacts, ordering, recovery, and one backend. Not "the" store, not indispensable, not novel.
4. **Decisions**: deterministic set first; one scorer port; shadow one semantic decision at a time; admission may be empty; adjudication carries uncertainty and review.
5. **Evidence**: consolidation claim narrowed and re-cited; statistics fixed in the discovery plan.
6. **Scope**: twelve-week pilot as the review sketches; full component map retained as the ecosystem description.
