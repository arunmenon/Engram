# Belief register

**Purpose.** Beliefs live here, separately from the pillar taxonomy, so that changing a belief never requires relabelling the archive and an agent never has to agree with a belief to classify an item. Each belief has an id, a qualified statement, the claims for and against it, an uncertainty grade, the decision it affects, linked experiments, and the last review date. Beliefs change only through **revision events** (`queue/beliefs/BR-YYYYMMDD-NN.json` on the bus), written by the hypotheses agent after a verdict or by a person at review. The contradictions table is generated from revision events.

**Uncertainty grades.** `firm` (replicated or contract-mandated), `working` (one good source or our own measurement), `lead` (field report or single unablated paper), `open` (no usable evidence).
